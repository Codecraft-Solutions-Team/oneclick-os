OneClick OS can show your OneDrive as a folder in **Files**, next to your own folders. There is no separate OneDrive app to install.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. The Cloud Drives page is new and has not been tested from start to finish on many PCs. It may not be finished. If it fails, please [report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).

## Connect

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Cloud Drives**.
2. On the **Microsoft OneDrive** card, press **Sign in to OneDrive**.
3. Your web browser opens on Microsoft's sign-in page. Sign in and allow access. If the browser does not open, press **Open Sign-in Page**.
4. Go back to the Cloud Drives page. When the label on the card reads **Ready in Files**, OneDrive is connected.
5. Press **Open in Files**.

Your OneDrive is now a folder called **OneDrive** in your Home folder. It is also pinned in the Files sidebar.

You type your Microsoft password on Microsoft's own page in the browser. OneClick OS does not see it and has no account system of its own.

## Use it

Open, copy, move and save files in the OneDrive folder as you would in any other folder. Changes go to OneDrive over the internet, so you need to be online. Large files take time to open because they are fetched when you ask for them.

## If the label says Connected but the folder is empty

**Connected** means you are signed in but the folder is not attached yet. Press **Connect Folder**. Use **Refresh** at the top of the page to check the state again.

## Disconnect

Press **Disconnect** on the OneDrive card. The folder is detached from this PC. Your files stay in OneDrive and nothing is deleted from your account.

## Without connecting

You can always use OneDrive in a web browser by signing in at Microsoft's site. This also works with [Office on the web](/apps/office-on-the-web).

For the whole page, see [The Cloud Drives page](/settings/cloud-drives-page). For Google's service, see [Connect Google Drive](/apps/google-drive).
