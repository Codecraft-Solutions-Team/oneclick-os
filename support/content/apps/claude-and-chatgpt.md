The Start menu has two shortcuts pinned by default, **Claude** and **ChatGPT**. This page explains exactly what they are, because it is easy to assume more.

## What they are

They are web shortcuts and nothing else.

- **Claude** opens the Claude website, made by Anthropic, in your web browser.
- **ChatGPT** opens the ChatGPT website, made by OpenAI, in your web browser.

Pressing one is the same as typing the address into the browser yourself.

## What they are not

- There is **no AI built into OneClick OS**. Nothing in the system reads your files, watches your screen, or listens.
- The shortcuts are **not apps**. Nothing extra is installed.
- OneClick OS is **not connected to either company** and sends them nothing. Until you press a shortcut and sign in, neither service knows this PC exists.
- They do **not include an account or a subscription**.

## What you need to use them

You need your own account with the service, and an internet connection. Each company decides what is free and what is paid, and each has its own privacy policy. What you type into those websites goes to that company, the same as on any other computer.

## Remove the shortcuts

If you do not want them in the Start menu:

1. Open the **Start menu**.
2. Right-click **Claude** or **ChatGPT**.
3. Choose **Unpin from Start**.

To put one back later, see [Pin apps to Start and the taskbar](/desktop/pin-apps).

For what OneClick OS does and does not send anywhere, see [What OneClick never does](/updates/what-oneclick-never-does) and [Privacy settings](/settings/privacy).
