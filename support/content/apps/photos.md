## View a photo

Open **Files**, go to your **Pictures** folder, and double-click a picture. It opens in the built-in image viewer. Use the arrow keys to move to the next or previous picture in the same folder.

Files also shows small previews of pictures, so you can often find the one you want without opening any.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. The image viewer has not been given a OneClick name or checked on every PC yet. If a picture opens in something unexpected, see [Choose which app opens a file](/apps/default-apps).

## Small edits

Image viewers usually have basic tools such as crop, rotate and resize. Look for an edit option in the viewer's toolbar or menu.

## Bigger edits

For more than a crop, install an editor from the App Store. These are free:

| App | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| GIMP | Photo editing, retouching, layers. The closest thing to Photoshop. |
| Krita | Drawing and digital painting |
| Inkscape | Logos and vector artwork, like Illustrator |

Open the **App Store**, search for the name, and press **Install**. They are also in the Creator starter set in the welcome tour.

Photoshop and the other Adobe apps do not run on OneClick OS. See [Photoshop and other Adobe apps](/windows/adobe).

## Get photos from a phone or camera

- **Camera or memory card.** Plug it in. It appears in Files like a USB stick. Copy the pictures to your Pictures folder. See [USB sticks and external drives](/desktop/external-drives).
- **Android phone.** Plug it in with a USB cable and choose file transfer on the phone when it asks.
- **iPhone.** This is more limited. See [iPhone, iTunes and iCloud](/windows/itunes-and-iphone).
- **Google Photos or iCloud Photos.** Use them in the web browser.
