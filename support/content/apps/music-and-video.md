## Play a file

Open **Files**, find the song or video, and double-click it. It opens in a media player.

If a file will not play, or nothing opens, install **VLC**. It is free and plays almost every kind of video and audio file.

1. Open the **App Store** from the Start menu.
2. Search for **VLC** and press **Install**.
3. Right-click the file in Files, choose **Open With**, and pick VLC.

To make VLC open that kind of file every time, see [Choose which app opens a file](/apps/default-apps).

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Which media player is present from the start has not been settled. VLC from the App Store is the dependable choice.

## Streaming

Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Stan, ABC iview and similar services work in the web browser. Sign in on their websites as you would on any computer.

> Note: Some streaming services limit picture quality in browsers on systems other than Windows and Mac. That is the service's decision, and OneClick OS cannot change it.

## Spotify and other music services

Spotify has an app in the App Store. Search for **Spotify** and press **Install**. It is also in the Everyday starter set in the welcome tour. Spotify and most other music services, such as YouTube Music and Apple Music, also work in the browser.

## iTunes

iTunes does not run on OneClick OS. Music files you bought or copied from CDs are ordinary files and play in any player once you have moved them across. See [iPhone, iTunes and iCloud](/windows/itunes-and-iphone).

## DVDs and Blu-ray

VLC can play many DVDs if your PC has a disc drive. Blu-ray discs bought in shops are copy-protected and usually do not play.

## No sound

If a video plays but you hear nothing, see [No sound](/troubleshooting/no-sound).
