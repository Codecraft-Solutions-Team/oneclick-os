title: Open, sign and print PDFs
summary: PDFs open in the built-in reader, where you can mark them up and print them.

You do not need Adobe Reader. OneClick OS opens PDF files with a built-in reader.

## Open a PDF

Double-click the file in **Files**. A PDF you click on a web page opens inside the web browser instead. To keep it, use the browser's download button, then open it from your **Downloads** folder.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. The PDF reader has not been given a OneClick name yet, and its menus may change. The steps below are kept general for that reason.

## Fill in a form

Many PDF forms have boxes you can type into. Click a box and type, then save the file. If you cannot click into the boxes, the form is only a picture of a form. In that case add your text with the reader's annotation tools.

## Sign a PDF

For most everyday paperwork, a signature you draw or place on the page is enough.

1. Open the PDF in the reader.
2. Look for the annotation tools in the toolbar or the menus.
3. Choose the freehand drawing tool and draw your signature with the mouse or touchpad.
4. Save the file, preferably under a new name so that you keep the unsigned original.

Your web browser can do the same. Firefox, for example, has drawing and text tools at the top of a PDF it has opened.

> Note: A drawn signature is a mark on the page. It is not a certified digital signature. If an organisation asks for one of those, or sends you a link to sign on a website, follow their instructions in the browser.

## Print a PDF

Open the PDF, then press **Ctrl + P**, or choose **Print** from the menu. Pick your printer and the pages you want. If no printer is listed, see [Add a printer](/settings/printers).

## Save something as a PDF

Most apps can make a PDF. In the print window, choose the option to print to a file or to PDF instead of a real printer. LibreOffice also has an export to PDF option in its **File** menu.

## Edit the text of a PDF

PDFs are made to be read, not edited. For small changes, LibreOffice can open a PDF in its drawing program. For anything larger, go back to the original document. See [Open and edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files](/apps/libreoffice).
