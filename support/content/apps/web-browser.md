The web browser is the first icon pinned in the Start menu and on the taskbar. On OneClick OS this is normally **Firefox**.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. If there is no browser in your Start menu, open the **App Store**, search for **Firefox**, and press **Install**.

## Use a different browser

You are not tied to one browser. Chrome, Brave and others are free in the App Store.

1. Open the **App Store** from the Start menu.
2. Search for the browser by name.
3. Press **Install**.

You can have more than one browser installed at the same time.

> Note: Microsoft Edge and Safari are made for other systems. If you used Edge on Windows, Chrome or Brave will feel the most familiar, and most Chrome extensions work in both.

## Make it the default

The default browser is the one that opens when you click a link in another app. It is also the one the first pinned icon opens.

Most browsers ask whether to become the default the first time you open them. Say yes. You can also set it from inside the browser, in its own settings, under a heading such as "Default browser".

The web shortcuts in the Start menu (Word on the web, Claude, ChatGPT, GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming) open in whichever browser is the default.

## Bring your bookmarks and passwords

The easiest way is to sign in to the same browser account you used on Windows, such as a Firefox account or a Google account in Chrome. Your bookmarks, passwords and history follow you. See [Move bookmarks and passwords](/windows/move-browser).

## Web apps

Many things that were programs on Windows are websites now. Email, Office, WhatsApp, Teams and most streaming services all work in the browser with nothing to install. See [Use Microsoft Office on the web](/apps/office-on-the-web).
