OneClick OS can show your Google Drive as a folder in **Files**. You set this up once in Settings.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. The Cloud Drives page is new and has not been tested from start to finish on many PCs. It may not be finished. If it fails, please [report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).

## Two ways to connect

Open **Settings** and choose **Cloud Drives**. The **Google Drive** card has two options.

- **Sign in with Google** signs in with your Google account. At the moment Google often refuses this with the message "This app is blocked". That is a known problem and not something you did wrong.
- **Direct Folder Sync** links Google Drive as a folder. This is the one to use if the first option is blocked.

## Link the folder

1. On the Google Drive card, under **Direct Folder Sync**, press **Link Google Drive Folder**.
2. Your web browser opens on Google's sign-in page. Sign in and allow access. If the browser does not open, press **Open Sign-in Page**.
3. Go back to the Cloud Drives page. When the label on the card reads **Folder Ready**, press **Open Google Drive Folder**.

Your drive is now a folder called **Google Drive** in your Home folder, and it is added to Files.

You type your Google password on Google's own page in the browser. OneClick OS does not see it.

## Use it

Work in the folder as you would in any other. You need to be online, because files are fetched from Google when you open them and sent back when you save.

Google Docs, Sheets and Slides documents are web documents, not ordinary files. The most reliable way to edit them is in the browser at Google's site.

## If the folder is not attached

If the card shows **Connected** but the folder is empty, press **Connect Folder**. Press **Refresh** at the top of the page to check again.

## Disconnect

Press **Disconnect Folder**. The folder is detached from this PC. Nothing is deleted from your Google account.
