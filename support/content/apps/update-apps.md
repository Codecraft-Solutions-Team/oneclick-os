Apps come from the **App Store** and are updated there when you choose. They do not update behind your back or restart your PC. System updates are separate and live in **Settings** under **Update**. See [How updates work](/updates/how-updates-work).

## Update apps in the App Store

1. Open the **App Store** from the Start menu.
2. Choose **Updates** to see which installed apps have a newer version.
3. Press **Update all**, or press **Update** next to a single app.

Apps you already have open keep running without losing your work. They switch to the new version the next time you close and reopen them.

> Note: You need an internet connection to check for and install app updates. The App Store is free and needs no account.

## What updates somewhere else

- **Built-in tools** such as Files, Settings, Task Manager, and the App Store itself are part of the system. They update when you update the system, not here.
- **Web shortcuts** such as Word on the web, Claude, ChatGPT, GeForce NOW, and Xbox Cloud Gaming open in your browser, so they stay current on their own websites.
- **Windows programs** added through **Bottles** do not appear in the App Store updates list. Update them inside the program itself or from **Bottles**. See [Run Windows programs](/apps/windows-programs).

## Still stuck?

If an app update fails, check your internet connection and try again, or see [Install apps from the App Store](/apps/install-apps) and [An app will not open](/troubleshooting/app-wont-open). If it still will not update, please [report the problem](/reports) and name the app.
