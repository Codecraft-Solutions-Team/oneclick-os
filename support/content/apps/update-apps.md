Apps come from the App Store and are updated there. System updates are separate, see [How updates work](/updates/how-updates-work).

## Update apps

1. Open the **App Store** from the Start menu.
2. Choose **Updates**.
3. Press **Update all**, or **Update** next to a single app.

Apps you have open keep running. They use the new version the next time you open them.
