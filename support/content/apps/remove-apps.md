Apps you installed from the App Store are removed in the App Store. There is no separate "Add or remove programs" list.

## Remove an app

1. Open the **App Store** from the Start menu.
2. Open the list of installed apps, or search for the app by name.
3. Choose the app.
4. Press **Remove**.

The app disappears from the Start menu. Files you made with the app, such as documents and pictures, stay in your folders.

## What you cannot remove this way

- **Built-in tools** such as Files, Settings, Task Manager and the App Store are part of the system. They are not removed from the App Store.
- **Web shortcuts** such as Word on the web, Claude, ChatGPT, GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming are not apps. They only open a website. If you do not want one in the Start menu, right-click it and choose **Unpin from Start**. See [Pin apps to Start and the taskbar](/desktop/pin-apps).

## Removing a Windows program

Windows programs that you installed through Bottles live inside Bottles, not in the App Store. Open Bottles to remove them. If you remove Bottles itself from the App Store, Windows programs stop opening until you install it again. See [Run Windows programs](/apps/windows-programs).

## Getting an app back

Search for it in the App Store and press **Install** again. See [Install apps from the App Store](/apps/install-apps).
