Apple makes iTunes, iCloud for Windows and the Apple Devices app for Windows and Mac only. None of them run on OneClick OS. Your iPhone keeps working exactly as before. What changes is how the PC and the phone share things.

> Note: This is an early test version. Nothing on this page has been tested with a real iPhone on a real PC yet.

## What works through the web

Sign in at [icloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) in your web browser to reach:

- iCloud Photos, to view and download your pictures
- iCloud Drive files
- Mail, Contacts, Calendar, Notes and Reminders
- Find My

Apple Music also has a web player you can sign in to.

## Getting photos off the phone

- **iCloud Photos** is the dependable route. Turn it on in the phone's settings and download from icloud.com.
- **A cable** may work. Plug the iPhone in, unlock it and tap **Trust** on the phone. It may then appear in **Files** like a camera, with your photos in folders. We have not tested this, so do not count on it.
- Another cloud service, such as Google Photos or OneDrive, also works. Install its app on the phone, let it upload, then open it on the PC.

## What does not work

- **Backing up the iPhone to the PC.** Use iCloud Backup on the phone, or a Windows PC or Mac.
- **Restoring or updating the iPhone from the PC.** The phone can update itself over Wi-Fi. A full restore needs a Windows PC or a Mac.
- **Syncing music or films from an iTunes library to the phone.**
- **iMessage and FaceTime on the PC.**

## Your iTunes music

Music files in your iTunes folder are ordinary files. Copy the folder across and play them with any music app. See [Play music and video](/apps/music-and-video).

Very old purchases from the iTunes Store may be copy-protected and will not play. Films and TV programmes bought from iTunes are protected and do not play outside Apple's apps.

## Before you leave Windows

1. Check that iCloud Backup is on, and that the last backup is recent.
2. Check that your photos are in iCloud or copied to a drive.
3. If you sync anything by cable with iTunes, decide how you will do without it.

If you depend on iTunes for backups or syncing, keep Windows beside OneClick OS. See [Keep Windows and install OneClick beside it](/getting-started/dual-boot-with-windows).
