title: Move your files from Windows
summary: There is no automatic transfer tool yet. Copy your files with an external drive, a cloud drive, or straight from the Windows disk.
order: 30
---
OneClick OS does not yet have a tool that moves your files across from Windows for you. That tool has not been started. For now you copy them yourself, which is a matter of dragging folders.

## With an external drive

This is the dependable way, and it doubles as your backup.

1. In Windows, copy your folders to an external drive or USB stick. See [Back up your files before installing](/getting-started/back-up-first) for what to copy.
2. In OneClick OS, plug the drive in. It appears in the sidebar of **Files**.
3. Open it, select what you want, and copy it into the matching folder in your Home folder: Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos or Downloads.

See [USB sticks and external drives](/desktop/external-drives).

## With a cloud drive

If your files are already in OneDrive or Google Drive, you do not need to copy anything by hand.

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Cloud Drives**.
2. Sign in to OneDrive or Google Drive.
3. Your cloud files appear as a folder in **Files**.

See [Connect OneDrive](/apps/onedrive) and [Connect Google Drive](/apps/google-drive).

## Straight from the Windows disk

If you kept Windows beside OneClick OS, or the old Windows disk is still in the PC, you can usually open it from **Files** and copy from it directly. See [Open your Windows drive from OneClick](/windows/read-windows-drives).

## What does and does not come across

Your files come across: documents, photos, music, videos.

These do not:

- **Programs.** They have to be installed again from the App Store, or run through Bottles if there is no version for OneClick OS. See [Run Windows programs](/apps/windows-programs).
- **Settings** inside programs.
- **Browser bookmarks and passwords**, unless you move them. See [Move bookmarks and passwords](/windows/move-browser).
- **Email stored only on the PC.** Email kept by your provider, such as Gmail or Outlook.com, shows up again when you sign in.

## Will my files open

Common file types open without any extra steps: photos, PDFs, music, video and Office documents. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files open in LibreOffice. See [Open and edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files](/apps/libreoffice).

Files made by a program that only exists for Windows may have nothing to open them. Check before you rely on it.
