Adobe does not make its desktop apps for this kind of system. Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, InDesign and the rest of Creative Cloud do not run on OneClick OS.

Running them through Bottles is not a dependable answer either. Do not plan your work around it.

## What you can use instead

These are free and in the **App Store**. Several can be ticked in the welcome tour under the **Creator** starter set.

| For | Try |
|---|---|
| Photo editing | GIMP |
| Drawing and painting | Krita |
| Vector graphics, logos | Inkscape |
| Video editing | Kdenlive |
| Sound editing | Audacity |
| 3D | Blender |

They are capable apps, but they are not copies of Adobe's. Menus, shortcuts and ways of working are different, and there is a learning period.

## Your existing files

- Common formats such as JPG, PNG, TIFF, SVG, MP4 and PDF open without trouble.
- Photoshop PSD files open in GIMP and Krita, but not perfectly. Simple layers come across. Smart objects, adjustment layers and some effects may not.
- Project files from Premiere Pro, After Effects and InDesign have nothing here that opens them. Export finished work to a common format in Windows before you switch.

## Adobe on the web

Adobe offers some of its tools as websites that run in a browser. Whether they suit you, and which plan they need, is for Adobe to say. Check [adobe.com](https://www.adobe.com). There are also other browser-based editors that need nothing installed.

## If you need Adobe for work

Be honest with yourself about this. If your job, your course or your clients need real Adobe files, keep Windows.

- Install OneClick OS beside Windows and restart into Windows for Adobe work. See [Keep Windows and install OneClick beside it](/getting-started/dual-boot-with-windows).
- Or keep Adobe on another computer.
