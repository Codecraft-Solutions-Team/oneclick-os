GeForce NOW is a cloud gaming service from Nvidia. The game runs on one of Nvidia's computers and the picture is sent to your screen over the internet. Because the game is not running on your PC, some games that cannot run on OneClick OS can be played this way.

## What the shortcut does

**GeForce NOW** is pinned in the Start menu. Pressing it opens [play.geforcenow.com](https://play.geforcenow.com) in your web browser. That is all it does. Nothing is installed, and OneClick OS has no connection to Nvidia's service.

## What you need

- **A GeForce NOW account.** Nvidia sets the plans and prices. Check their site for what is free and what is paid.
- **The games themselves.** GeForce NOW does not give you games. You link your Steam, Epic or other store account and play games you already own, where the service carries them.
- **A good internet connection.** A wired connection or strong Wi-Fi makes a large difference.

You do not need an Nvidia graphics card. Any PC that can play video smoothly will do.

## Start playing

1. Open the **Start menu** and choose **GeForce NOW**.
2. Sign in or create an account on Nvidia's page.
3. Search for a game, connect the store account you own it on, and press play.

## If the page does not work in your browser

GeForce NOW is built mainly for Chrome and browsers based on it. If the page says your browser is not supported, or the stream will not start, install **Google Chrome** from the App Store and open [play.geforcenow.com](https://play.geforcenow.com) there. See [Your web browser](/apps/web-browser).

## Games blocked by anti-cheat

Some games that refuse to run on OneClick OS are available on GeForce NOW. Not all are, and the list changes. Look the game up on Nvidia's site before relying on it. See [Why some online games will not run](/gaming/anti-cheat).

> Note: Picture quality and delay depend on your internet connection and on Nvidia's service. OneClick OS cannot improve either.

To remove the shortcut, right-click it in the Start menu and choose **Unpin from Start**. See also [Xbox Cloud Gaming](/gaming/xbox-cloud-gaming).
