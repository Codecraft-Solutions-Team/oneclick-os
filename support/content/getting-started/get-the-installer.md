> Warning: The installer is not available yet. On the download page both buttons say "Installer not ready yet". This page explains how it will work when they are switched on.

## Where it comes from

The installer is only offered on the [Download page](https://oneclickos.org/download). It is free and you do not need an account. Do not download it from anywhere else.

## Before the buttons work

1. Read the box at the top of the Download page, and the [Test version notice](https://oneclickos.org/test-version).
2. Back up anything you cannot lose. See [Back up your files before installing](/getting-started/back-up-first).
3. Tick the box to say you have read the notice, the Terms of use and the Privacy policy.

## Pick a version

There are two:

| Version | Choose it if |
|---|---|
| **Most PCs** | Your PC has Intel or AMD graphics, or you are not sure. |
| **PCs with Nvidia graphics** | Your gaming PC or laptop has an Nvidia card. |

See [Which version for Nvidia graphics](/getting-started/nvidia-version) for how to check.

## Choose apps first, if you like

The [Apps page](https://oneclickos.org/apps) lets you tick the apps you want before you download. Your ticks stay in your own browser and are not sent to us. Installing those picks on first start is still being built, so do not rely on it yet. You can always choose apps later. See [Choose your starter apps](/getting-started/choose-starter-apps).

## After the download

The installer is one large file. It does nothing by being opened in Windows. It has to be put on a USB stick, and the PC has to be started from that stick.

1. [Put the installer on a USB stick](/getting-started/make-the-installer-usb)
2. [Start your PC from the USB stick](/getting-started/start-from-usb)
3. [Install OneClick OS on a PC](/getting-started/install-on-a-pc)
