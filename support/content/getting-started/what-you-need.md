> Warning: The installer for PCs is not available yet. This list describes what you will need once it is released.

## The checklist

- **A PC with an Intel or AMD processor.** That covers most desktop and laptop PCs sold with Windows. See [Will it run on my PC](/getting-started/is-my-pc-supported).
- **A USB stick of 16 GB or more.** Everything on the stick is erased when the installer is put on it.
- **A backup of your files.** Keep it on a separate drive or in the cloud, not on the PC you are installing on. See [Back up your files before installing](/getting-started/back-up-first).
- **An internet connection.** You need it to download the installer, and afterwards to install apps and updates.
- **A second device.** A phone is enough. It lets you read these pages while the PC is busy.
- **Time.** Set aside an unhurried afternoon. Do not start an hour before you need the PC for something.

## If you plan to keep Windows

- Make a Windows recovery drive first. Search for **Recovery Drive** in the Windows Start menu.
- If your Windows drive uses BitLocker or device encryption, find your recovery key and write it down before you change anything.
- Read [Keep Windows and install OneClick beside it](/getting-started/dual-boot-with-windows).

## Things worth noting down first

- Your Wi-Fi name and password.
- The sign-in details for your email, your browser and anything else you use. A new system will not know them.
- Licence keys for any paid programs you may want to reinstall on Windows later.
- Which programs and games you rely on. Check them against [What does not work, and what to use instead](/windows/what-does-not-work).

## What you do not need

- You do not need an account with us.
- You do not need to pay anything.
- You do not need to download drivers. See [Do I need to install drivers](/windows/drivers).

> Note: This is an early test version. Do not install it on a computer you depend on for work or school.
