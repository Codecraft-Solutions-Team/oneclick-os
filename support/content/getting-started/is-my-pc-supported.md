title: Will it run on my PC
summary: Which PCs OneClick OS is made for, and why nobody can promise yours yet.

---
The honest answer today is that we do not know yet. OneClick OS is an early test version. So far it has been run on one virtual machine and on no real PCs. The installer for PCs is not available yet.

## PCs it is made for

- Desktop and laptop PCs with an **Intel or AMD processor**. This is nearly every PC that came with Windows 10 or 11.
- PCs with Intel, AMD or Nvidia graphics. Nvidia has its own version. See [Which version for Nvidia graphics](/getting-started/nvidia-version).

## PCs it is not made for

- PCs with an **Arm processor**, such as Windows laptops with a Snapdragon chip.
- Macs. There is no installer for Mac hardware.
- Tablets and phones.

## How to check your processor in Windows

1. Open **Settings** in Windows.
2. Choose **System**, then **About**.
3. Look at **Processor**. A name that starts with Intel or AMD is the right kind.

## Parts that may behave differently

Even on a suitable PC, some hardware may not work, or may work differently from Windows:

- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
- Sound
- Graphics
- Fingerprint readers
- Sleep and battery life

Most common hardware has drivers built in, but a few devices have none at all. See [Do I need to install drivers](/windows/drivers).

## The safest way to find out

When the installer is released, try OneClick OS on a spare PC first, or install it to an external USB drive so your own disk is never touched. That shows you what works on your hardware before you commit.

If something on your PC does not work, please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report). Reports from real PCs are how the list of working hardware gets built. See [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).
