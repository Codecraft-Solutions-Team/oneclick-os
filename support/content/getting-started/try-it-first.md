You can get a feel for OneClick OS without installing anything. The home page at [oneclickos.org](https://oneclickos.org) has a working model of the desktop that runs in your web browser.

## What you can do in the model

1. Open [oneclickos.org](https://oneclickos.org) on any computer.
2. Scroll to the desktop model.
3. Click things. You can open the Start menu, drag windows around, change the taskbar in Settings, and run a pretend update.

Nothing is downloaded and nothing on your computer changes.

## What the model cannot tell you

The model is a copy of how the desktop looks and behaves. It is not the real system running on your PC, so it cannot answer these questions:

- Whether your Wi-Fi, sound, graphics and other hardware will work.
- Whether a particular game or program will run.
- How fast it will feel on your PC.

> Note: Some parts shown on the website are still being built and may be missing from the test version. The home page keeps a plain list of what is working, what is being built and what has not been started.

## The next step

When the installer is released, the safest way to try the real thing will be on a spare PC, or on an external USB drive so your own disk is never touched. The installer is not available yet.
