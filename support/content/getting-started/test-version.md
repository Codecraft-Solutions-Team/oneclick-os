OneClick OS is in early development. What is offered is a test version, sometimes called a pre-release or alpha. It exists so people can try it and tell us what is broken. It is not a finished product.

The installer for PCs is not available yet. The full wording is in the [Test version notice](https://oneclickos.org/test-version). This page is the plain summary.

## What can go wrong

- **You can lose data.** Installing changes the disk you install on. Replacing Windows, or picking the wrong disk, erases everything on that disk.
- **It may not start.** It has been run on very few computers. It may fail to install, fail to start, or stop working after an update.
- **Hardware may not work.** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound, graphics, fingerprint readers, sleep and battery life can all behave differently from Windows, or not work at all.
- **Apps and games may not run.** Some software is made only for Windows. See [What does not work, and what to use instead](/windows/what-does-not-work).
- **Features may be missing.** Parts described on the website or in these help pages are still being built and may be absent or different.
- **Updates can change things.** A test update can remove, rename or break something that worked. You can go back. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).
- **It has had no security review.** Do not rely on it to protect sensitive information.

## What we ask you to do

- Back up everything you cannot afford to lose, to a separate drive or the cloud. See [Back up your files before installing](/getting-started/back-up-first).
- Do not install it on a computer you need for work, study or anything urgent.
- Prefer a spare computer, or an external USB drive, so your own disk is never touched.
- If you install beside Windows, make a Windows recovery drive first.
- If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before installing.

## What is not promised

Bug reports are read, and we try to fix what is reported. We do not promise a reply, a fix or a date. There is no helpline.

## How to help

When something breaks, [report a problem](https://oneclickos.org/report). You do not need an account. Reports are what turn a test version into a finished one. See [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) for what to include.
