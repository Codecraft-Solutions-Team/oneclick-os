After first-time setup, a window called **Welcome** opens. It is a short tour with a few pages. You can close it at any point, and nothing in it is required.

## The pages

### Welcome

A short greeting. It tells you the tour shows where things are.

### Where things are

A picture of the desktop with three parts marked:

1. The **Start menu**, at the bottom left. Press the **Windows key** or click Start. Start typing at any time to search for apps, files and Settings pages.
2. The **taskbar** along the bottom, with your open windows and pinned apps.
3. Wi-Fi, sound and the clock, at the bottom right.

It also reminds you that **Ctrl+Shift+Esc** opens Task Manager. Two buttons, **Open Settings** and **Taskbar Settings**, take you straight there.

### Your apps

Pick apps to install, one by one or as a starter set. See [Choose your starter apps](/getting-started/choose-starter-apps).

### Updates are your choice

Explains that nothing updates by itself, that you can choose full updates or security fixes only, and that the last update can be undone. The **Open Update Settings** button opens the Update page. See [How updates work](/updates/how-updates-work).

### Coming from Windows

A plain list of what works right away and what does not, with what to use instead. Two buttons, **Open Windows Apps Settings** and **Open Cloud Drives Settings**, open those pages. See [What does not work, and what to use instead](/windows/what-does-not-work).

## Open the tour again

1. Open the **Start menu**.
2. Type **Welcome**.
3. Open the **Welcome** app.
