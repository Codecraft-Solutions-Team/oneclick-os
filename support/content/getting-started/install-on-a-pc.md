> Warning: The installer is not available yet. The Download page says "Installer not ready yet". This page describes how installing will work. The installer screens are not final, so the steps are kept general.

## Before you begin

- You have a backup on a separate drive. See [Back up your files before installing](/getting-started/back-up-first).
- You have read [What "early test version" means](/getting-started/test-version).
- This is not a PC you depend on for work or school.
- A laptop is plugged into power.

## The steps

1. [Get the installer](/getting-started/get-the-installer) and [put it on a USB stick](/getting-started/make-the-installer-usb).
2. [Start your PC from the USB stick](/getting-started/start-from-usb).
3. Answer the installer's questions. It asks about four things: your language, your keyboard, the disk to install on, and your name.
4. Confirm, and wait while it installs.
5. When it finishes, restart and remove the USB stick when told to.

## The disk question

This is the one answer that matters. Take your time over it.

- **Replace Windows** erases everything on the disk you choose. Windows, your programs and your files on that disk are gone and cannot be recovered.
- **Keep Windows** installs OneClick OS beside it, and you choose which to use each time the PC starts. See [Keep Windows and install OneClick beside it](/getting-started/dual-boot-with-windows).

If the PC has more than one disk, check the name and size of the one you pick. Unplug external drives you do not want touched, including your backup drive.

> Warning: Until you confirm the disk choice, nothing has changed. If you are unsure, turn the PC off, remove the stick, and Windows starts as before.

## After the restart

The PC starts OneClick OS for the first time. You see the name being written on screen, then a short setup. See [First-time setup, page by page](/getting-started/first-run-setup).

## If something goes wrong

- The PC will not start afterwards: see [The PC will not start OneClick OS](/troubleshooting/wont-start).
- Anything else: please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report). The test version has been run on very few computers, and your report helps the next person.
