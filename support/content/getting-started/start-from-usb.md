> Warning: The installer is not available yet. These steps describe how it will work.

A PC normally starts from its own disk. To run the installer, you tell it to start from the USB stick once.

## From inside Windows 11

1. Plug in the installer stick.
2. Open **Settings**, then **System**, then **Recovery**.
3. Next to **Advanced startup**, press **Restart now**.
4. On the blue screen, choose **Use a device**, then pick the USB stick.

If **Use a device** is not offered, use the key method below.

## With a key at power-on

1. Turn the PC off completely.
2. Plug in the installer stick.
3. Turn the PC on and tap the start-up menu key again and again until a short list of devices appears.
4. Use the arrow keys to pick the USB stick and press **Enter**.

The key depends on who made the PC. **F12**, **F9**, **F8**, **Esc** and **F2** are common. Many PCs show the key for a moment on the first screen. If none work, search the web for your PC's model and "boot menu key".

## If Windows starts anyway

- You tapped too late. Shut down and try again, tapping from the moment you press the power button.
- Try a different USB port. Ports on the PC itself work better than hubs.
- Windows may not have fully shut down. In Windows, hold **Shift** while you choose **Restart**, then use **Use a device**.
- The stick may not have been written correctly. See [Put the installer on a USB stick](/getting-started/make-the-installer-usb).

> Note: Some PCs have a security setting in their start-up settings that can refuse to start from a USB stick. How the OneClick installer behaves on those PCs is not yet known. If your PC refuses the stick, please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report).

## What you should see

The installer starts and asks its first question. Nothing on your disk has changed yet. To stop here, turn the PC off and pull out the stick. Windows starts as before.
