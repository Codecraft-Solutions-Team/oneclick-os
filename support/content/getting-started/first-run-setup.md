The first time OneClick OS starts, a short setup runs before the desktop appears. It asks only what the PC needs to know. Nothing here signs you in to anything online.

> Note: This is an early test version. The pages may come in a slightly different order from the one below, and the wording may change.

## Language

The page says **Choose your language**. Pick the language you want for menus and settings. There is a search box if the list is long.

## Keyboard

The page says **Choose your keyboard layout**. Pick the layout that matches the keys in front of you, so that every key types what is printed on it. You can search here too.

## Wi-Fi

The page says **Connect to Wi-Fi**. Pick your network and enter its password. You can skip this page and connect from the taskbar later. See [Connect to Wi-Fi](/settings/wifi).

If the PC uses a network cable, there is nothing to do.

## Your account

The page says **Create your PC account**. You fill in:

- **Full Name**
- **Username**, a short name with no spaces
- **Password**
- **Confirm Password**

The account is stored on this PC only. No online account or sign-in is needed, and nothing is sent to us.

This first account has administrator permission. That means it can change system settings, install software and manage updates. You are asked for this password when you do those things, so choose one you will remember. There is no online reset. See [I forgot my password](/troubleshooting/forgot-password).

## Time

The page says **Set your time zone**. Pick your city or region so the taskbar clock is right.

## Finished

A last page tells you setup is complete. The desktop then opens, followed by the [welcome tour](/getting-started/welcome-tour).

## Changing these later

Everything you chose can be changed in **Settings**:

- [Keyboard layout and language](/settings/keyboard-and-language)
- [Date, time and time zone](/settings/date-and-time)
- [Change your password](/settings/password)
- [Add another person to this PC](/settings/users)
