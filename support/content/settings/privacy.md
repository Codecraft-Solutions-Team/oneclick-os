OneClick OS does not collect information about you or your PC. There is no account with us, no advertising identifier, no usage statistics and no crash reports sent to us.

## Why there is no feedback page

The desktop OneClick OS is built on normally has a page for sending usage information to its makers. OneClick removes that page and fixes the setting to off. That is why you will not find a "send usage data" switch in Settings. There is nothing to turn off.

When an app closes unexpectedly, the window that appears offers **Reopen**, **Copy Details** and **Close**. Nothing is uploaded. The details go somewhere only if you copy them and send them yourself.

No AI features are built into the system. The Claude and ChatGPT shortcuts open those websites in a browser and do nothing until you use them. See [The Claude and ChatGPT shortcuts](/apps/claude-and-chatgpt).

## What does go online

Some features have to contact a service to work. That service sees your internet address, as any website does.

- **Updates.** Checking for and downloading an update contacts the service that stores OneClick's system versions. This happens only when you check or download.
- **The App Store.** Listing and downloading apps contacts the app catalogue.
- **The clock and connection check.** Setting the time and testing for internet contact standard servers.

## Apps have their own rules

Apps you install, such as a web browser, a game launcher or a chat app, have their own privacy policies and settings. OneClick OS does not control what they collect. Check the settings inside each app.

## Things you can set

- A screen lock and a password, so others cannot open your session. See [Lock, sign out, restart and shut down](/desktop/lock-sign-out-shut-down).
- Separate accounts for each person. See [Add another person to this PC](/settings/users).
- Camera and microphone permission for websites, inside your web browser.

For the full list of promises, see [What OneClick never does](/updates/what-oneclick-never-does). The privacy policy is on [oneclickos.org](https://oneclickos.org).
