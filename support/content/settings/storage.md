## See how much space is left

Open **Files**. The amount of free space is shown for the place you are looking at. See [Find and manage your files](/desktop/files).

## See what is taking the space

OneClick OS has an app called **Disk Usage** that draws your folders as a ring chart. Bigger slices are bigger folders.

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `disk usage`.
2. Open **Disk Usage**.
3. Choose your Home folder to scan it.
4. Point at a slice to see its name and size. Select it to look inside.

> Note: If Disk Usage is not in your Start menu, it is not part of the version you have. Search for a disk usage app in the App Store instead.

## What usually fills a disk

- **Downloads.** Installers and files you only needed once.
- **Videos and games.** A single game can be tens of gigabytes.
- **Trash.** Deleted files stay there, still taking space, until you empty it.
- **Apps you no longer use.** See [Remove an app](/apps/remove-apps).

## What not to delete

Only delete things inside your own Home folder: Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos and the Desktop. Leave folders you do not recognise alone, and do not delete anything outside your Home folder. The system looks after its own files.

> Warning: Folders whose names start with a dot hold your app settings. Deleting them can reset or break an app.

## Earlier versions of the system

Each update keeps the version you had before, so you can go back. That takes some space, and it is managed for you. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

If the disk is already full and things are going wrong, see [The disk is full](/troubleshooting/out-of-space).
