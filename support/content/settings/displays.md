The display settings control resolution, the size of everything on screen, and how more than one screen is laid out.

## Open the display settings

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `display`.
2. Choose **Display & Monitor**.

## Change the resolution

Pick your screen, then choose a resolution from the list. The highest one listed is usually the screen's own resolution and gives the sharpest picture. Apply the change. If the picture looks wrong, you are asked whether to keep it. Wait without pressing anything and it goes back.

On the same page you can set the refresh rate and turn the picture sideways.

## Make everything bigger

Use the scale setting on this page. See [Make text and everything bigger](/settings/text-size).

## Add a second monitor

1. Plug the monitor in and turn it on. It is normally picked up straight away.
2. Open the display settings. Both screens are shown as boxes.
3. Drag the boxes so they match where the screens sit on your desk. The mouse then moves between them the right way.
4. Choose which one is the main screen. The taskbar goes there.

You can also choose to show the same picture on both screens, which is useful for a projector.

## If a screen is not found

- Check the cable at both ends and that the monitor is set to the right input.
- Try a different cable or socket.
- Restart with the monitor connected and switched on.
