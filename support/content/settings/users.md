Each person who uses the PC can have their own account. They get their own files, desktop, saved passwords and settings, and they cannot open each other's files.

Accounts live on this PC only. OneClick OS does not need an online account, an email address or a sign-in to us.

## Add an account

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `users`.
2. Choose **Users**.
3. Choose to add a new user.
4. Enter their name and a password.
5. Choose the type of account.
6. Confirm with your own password.

## Standard or administrator

| Type | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Use apps, save files and change their own settings |
| **Administrator** | All of that, plus change the system: updates, printers, other people's accounts |

Choose standard for children and for anyone who only needs to use the PC. Keep at least one administrator account that you know the password for.

> Note: It is worth having a second administrator account on a shared PC. If one person forgets their password, the other can change it. See [I forgot my password](/troubleshooting/forgot-password).

## Switch between people

Open the **Start menu**. Your name is at the bottom, with **Lock**, **Sign out**, **Restart** and **Shut down**. Choose **Sign out**, then the other person picks their name on the sign-in screen. See [Lock, sign out, restart and shut down](/desktop/lock-sign-out-shut-down).

## Remove an account

Open the Users page, select the account and remove it. You are asked whether to keep or delete that person's files.

> Warning: Deleting a person's files cannot be undone. Copy anything worth keeping first.

To change a password, see [Change your password](/settings/password).
