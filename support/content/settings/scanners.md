Scanning is done with a scanning app. Many scanners and all-in-one printers then work without installing a driver.

## Get a scanning app

We cannot confirm that a scanning app comes installed in this test version, so check first.

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `scan`.
2. If a scanning app appears, open it.
3. If nothing appears, open the **App Store**, search for `scan` and install a document scanning app.

See [Install apps from the App Store](/apps/install-apps).

## Scan a page

The exact buttons depend on the app, but the steps are the same in all of them.

1. Turn the scanner on. Connect it by USB, or make sure it is on the same network as the PC.
2. Open the scanning app. It looks for scanners and lists the ones it finds.
3. Choose your scanner.
4. Put the page face down on the glass.
5. Choose colour or black and white, then scan.
6. Save the result. Choose PDF for documents and JPG or PNG for photos.

To put several pages into one PDF, scan each page before saving. Most scanning apps keep the pages together until you save.

## If the scanner is not found

- Turn the scanner off and on again, then reopen the app.
- For a network scanner, check that it is on the same network as the PC.
- Try a USB cable instead of the network.
- Check that the printing side works. See [Add a printer](/settings/printers).

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Some scanners depend on the maker's Windows software and will not work. If yours is not found, please [report it](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model.

A phone camera with a scanning app is a good stand-in until then. To work with the result, see [Open, sign and print PDFs](/apps/pdf).
