## Dark mode

OneClick OS uses a dark look from the start: a navy background with white text and teal highlights. There is nothing to turn on.

Many apps follow the system and appear dark too. Some apps, and most websites, have their own light or dark setting inside the app.

Settings has a colours page where a different colour scheme can be chosen. OneClick's own windows, Start menu and Settings pages are designed for the dark look, so parts of the desktop may not match if you choose a light one.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. A finished light look is not part of it yet.

## Night light

Night light makes the screen warmer and less blue. Many people find this easier on the eyes in the evening.

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `night`.
2. Choose **Night Light**.
3. Turn it on.
4. Choose when it runs: all the time, at times you set, or from sunset to sunrise.
5. Set how warm the screen becomes.

The change in colour is gradual, so you may not notice the moment it starts.

> Note: Turn night light off when you are editing photos or video. It changes how colours look on screen, though not in the files.
