## Wired internet

Plug a network cable from your router into the PC. There is nothing to set up. The connection starts within a few seconds and the network icon beside the clock changes to show it.

If nothing happens:

- Push both ends of the cable in until they click.
- Try another socket on the router, or another cable.
- Restart the PC with the cable connected.

A wired connection is a good test when Wi-Fi is giving trouble. If the cable works and Wi-Fi does not, the problem is with Wi-Fi and not with your internet. See [Wi-Fi is missing or will not connect](/troubleshooting/no-wifi).

## Add a VPN

A VPN sends your internet traffic through another network, usually your workplace or a paid VPN service. You need the connection details from whoever runs it.

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `network`.
2. Choose **Wi-Fi & Network**.
3. Add a new connection and choose the VPN type your provider uses.
4. Enter the details they gave you, or import the settings file if they gave you one.
5. Save it. The VPN then appears with your other connections and can be turned on from the network icon beside the clock.

> Note: Which VPN types are offered depends on what is included in this test version. If the type you need is not in the list, please [report it](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).

## VPN apps from a provider

Some VPN companies offer their own app. Look for it in the **App Store** first. A Windows installer from the company's website will not set up a VPN on OneClick OS. See [Why you do not download installers from websites](/windows/installing-is-different).
