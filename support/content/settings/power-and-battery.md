## Open the power settings

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `power`.
2. Choose **Power Management**.

On a laptop there are separate settings for running on mains power, on battery and on low battery. A desktop PC has one set.

## What you can change

- When the screen dims and turns off.
- When the PC goes to sleep after sitting unused.
- What happens when you close a laptop lid.
- What the power button does.

Apply your changes when you are done.

## Sleep and shut down

**Sleep** keeps your apps open and uses very little power. Press a key or the power button to wake the PC. **Shut down** closes everything and turns the PC off.

Both are in the **Start menu**, at the bottom beside your name. See [Lock, sign out, restart and shut down](/desktop/lock-sign-out-shut-down).

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. On some PCs sleep does not work properly yet: the PC may not wake, or may wake with a black screen. If that happens, hold the power button until it turns off, start it again, and set the PC to never sleep until it is fixed. Please [report it](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).

## Battery

Choose the battery icon beside the clock to see how much charge is left. The same panel lets you stop the PC from sleeping for a while, which is useful during a film or a long download.

To make a battery last longer:

- Turn the screen brightness down. The screen is the largest drain.
- Let the screen turn off sooner.
- Close apps you are not using. See [Task Manager](/desktop/task-manager).

> Note: Battery life can be shorter or longer than it was on Windows, depending on the laptop.
