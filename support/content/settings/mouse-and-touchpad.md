## Open the settings

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `mouse`.
2. Choose **Mouse & Touchpad**.

A mouse and a touchpad have separate settings, so changing one does not change the other. The touchpad settings only appear on a PC that has a touchpad.

## Mouse

- **Pointer speed** sets how far the pointer moves when you move the mouse.
- **Pointer acceleration** makes fast movements travel further. Many people who play games turn it off.
- **Left-handed** swaps the left and right buttons.
- **Scroll direction** can be reversed if the wheel feels backwards to you.

## Touchpad

- **Tap to click** lets a light tap count as a click, without pressing down.
- **Two-finger scrolling** moves the page when you slide two fingers.
- **Scroll direction** can be set so the page follows your fingers, as on a phone, or moves the other way, as on most Windows laptops.
- **Right-click** can be a tap with two fingers or a press in the bottom-right corner.
- You can turn the touchpad off while you type or while a mouse is plugged in.

Changes take effect once you apply them. Try each one before moving on.

## Double-click

OneClick OS opens files and folders with a double-click, the same as Windows. A single click selects.

## If the pointer does not move

- For a wireless mouse, check the batteries and that its small USB receiver is plugged in.
- Try another USB socket.
- On a laptop, look for a key that turns the touchpad off and on. It often has a touchpad picture on it.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Some touchpads work only in a basic way, without gestures, and a few are not supported yet. Please [report it](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) if yours is one of them.
