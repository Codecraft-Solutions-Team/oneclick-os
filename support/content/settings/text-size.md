title: Make text and everything bigger
summary: Use display scaling to make text, icons and buttons larger.
order: 60
---
If things on screen are too small, the best fix is to scale the whole display. This makes text, icons, buttons and windows larger together, and keeps them sharp.

## Scale the whole screen

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `display`.
2. Choose **Display & Monitor**.
3. Find the scale setting and raise it, for example from 100% to 125% or 150%.
4. Apply the change.

Some apps only pick up the new size after you close and reopen them. If something still looks the wrong size, sign out and back in.

> Warning: Do not lower the resolution to make things bigger. It makes everything blurry. Leave the resolution at the screen's own and change the scale.

## Make the taskbar bigger

The taskbar has its own size setting.

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Taskbar**.
2. Under **Size**, choose **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**.

The change shows straight away. See [Change how the taskbar behaves](/desktop/taskbar-settings).

## Only the text

Settings also has a page for fonts, where the size of the text alone can be raised. Use this if you want larger words without larger buttons. Raising it a long way can make text overflow in some windows, so scaling the whole display is the safer choice.

## Zoom in a single app

Most apps can zoom their own content. In a web browser, hold **Ctrl** and press **+** to zoom in, **-** to zoom out and **0** to go back to normal.

If the screen is blurry or the wrong shape, see [The screen is the wrong size or blurry](/troubleshooting/screen-wrong-size).
