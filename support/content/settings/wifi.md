You can join a Wi-Fi network from the taskbar or from Settings. Both do the same thing.

## Connect from the taskbar

1. Look at the icons beside the clock and choose the network icon.
2. Pick your network from the list.
3. Enter the Wi-Fi password and confirm.

The PC remembers the network and joins it again by itself next time.

## Connect from Settings

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `wifi`.
2. Choose **Wi-Fi & Network**.
3. Pick your network and enter the password.

You were also offered a Wi-Fi step during first-time setup. If you joined a network there, it is already saved.

## Forget or change a network

Open the same page in Settings. Saved networks are listed there. Select one to change its password or remove it.

## If no networks are listed

- Check the Wi-Fi switch or key on your laptop, and that aeroplane mode is off.
- Restart the PC and look again.
- If there is still no Wi-Fi at all, the PC's Wi-Fi part may not be supported yet.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Most Wi-Fi works without any setup, but some models do not work yet.

For more help, see [Wi-Fi is missing or will not connect](/troubleshooting/no-wifi). To use a cable instead, see [Wired internet and VPN](/settings/wired-and-vpn).
