The Cloud Drives page connects your online storage so your files appear inside **Files**, next to the folders on the PC.

> Note: This page is new. It has been checked on screen, but signing in and opening real files has not been tested from start to finish. Parts of it may not work yet. Please [report](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) what you find.

## Open it

Open **Settings** and choose **Cloud Drives**. You can also type `cloud` in the Start menu.

The page has a **Refresh** button at the top and a section for each service.

## Microsoft OneDrive

1. Press **Sign in to OneDrive**.
2. A browser window opens. Sign in to Microsoft there and allow access. If no window opens, press **Open Sign-in Page**. Press **Cancel** to stop.
3. When sign-in finishes, the page shows that OneDrive is connected, and it is pinned in the Files sidebar.
4. Press **Open in Files** to see your files.

If the page says it is connected but the folder is not there yet, press **Connect Folder**.

To stop using it on this PC, press **Disconnect**. Your files stay in OneDrive.

## Google Drive

There are two ways to connect.

- **Sign in with Google** uses the standard sign-in. Google sometimes refuses it with the message "This app is blocked".
- **Direct Folder Sync** is the one to use if that happens. Press **Link Google Drive Folder**, sign in in the browser window that opens, then press **Open Google Drive Folder**.

To remove it, press **Disconnect Folder**.

## Good to know

- Your password is typed into Microsoft's or Google's own page in the browser, not into OneClick OS.
- The files stay online and are fetched when you open them, so you need an internet connection.
- If something fails, a message appears under the service. Read it, check your internet and try again.

For more detail, see [Connect OneDrive](/apps/onedrive) and [Connect Google Drive](/apps/google-drive).
