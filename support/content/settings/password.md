Your password is used to sign in, to unlock the screen, and to approve changes to the system such as restarting into an update.

## Change your own password

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `users`.
2. Choose **Users**.
3. Select your own account.
4. Choose to change the password.
5. Enter the new password twice and confirm.

The new password is used from the next time you sign in or unlock.

## The saved passwords prompt

OneClick OS keeps saved Wi-Fi and app passwords in a protected store, which appears in the Start menu as **Passwords**. After you change your sign-in password, you may be asked for the old one once so that store can be opened. Enter the old password when asked.

## Change someone else's password

An administrator can set a new password for another person.

1. Open the **Users** page.
2. Select their account.
3. Set a new password and confirm with your own.

Tell them the new password and ask them to change it to one only they know.

## Choose a good password

- Longer is better. Three or four unrelated words are easier to remember than a short jumble.
- Do not reuse a password you use on a website.
- Write it down and keep it somewhere safe at home. That is safer than forgetting it.

> Warning: OneClick OS has no online account, so there is no "email me a reset link". If you forget the password and nobody else has an administrator account on the PC, getting back in is difficult. See [I forgot my password](/troubleshooting/forgot-password).
