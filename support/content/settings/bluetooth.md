Bluetooth connects wireless headphones, speakers, mice, keyboards and game controllers.

## Pair a device

1. Put the device into pairing mode. This is usually done by holding a button until a light flashes. The device's manual says how.
2. Open the **Start menu**, type `bluetooth` and choose **Bluetooth**.
3. Make sure Bluetooth is turned on.
4. Choose to add a device, then pick yours from the list.
5. If you are asked to confirm a number or type a code, do so.

After pairing, the device reconnects by itself when it is switched on and in range.

You can also reach Bluetooth from the icons beside the clock.

## Disconnect or remove a device

Open the Bluetooth page in Settings. Paired devices are listed there. Select one to disconnect it for now, or remove it so the PC forgets it.

## Headphones and speakers

Pairing headphones does not always switch the sound to them. If you hear nothing, choose them as the output in the sound settings. See [Sound, speakers and microphones](/settings/sound).

## If the PC has no Bluetooth

Not every PC has Bluetooth built in. Many desktop PCs need a small USB Bluetooth adapter.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Most Bluetooth adapters work, but not all of them, and some devices pair on Windows and not here yet.

If a device will not pair or keeps dropping out, see [Bluetooth will not connect](/troubleshooting/bluetooth-not-working).
