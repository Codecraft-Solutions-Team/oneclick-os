title: An app closed unexpectedly
summary: What the window with Reopen, Copy Details and Close means, and what to do next.

---
When an app stops working and closes by itself, OneClick OS shows a small window that says the app "closed unexpectedly". Nothing is wrong with the PC. One app has failed, and the rest keeps running.

## What the buttons do

- **Reopen** starts the app again.
- **Close** closes the window and does nothing else.
- **Copy Details** copies the technical details so you can paste them into a report. When it has worked, the button says **Copied**.

There is also a line called **Details for developers**. Choose it to see the technical details. You do not need to read them.

If the window has no way to reopen the app, it tells you to open the app again from the Start menu.

> Note: Nothing is sent anywhere. OneClick OS does not upload crash reports. The details leave your PC only if you copy them and send them yourself.

## Did I lose my work

Anything you had not saved in that app may be gone. Some apps, such as LibreOffice and web browsers, offer to bring back what you were doing when you open them again. Files you had already saved are safe.

## If it keeps happening

1. Restart the PC and try again.
2. Update the app. Open the **App Store** and look under **Updates**. See [Update your apps](/apps/update-apps).
3. Think about what you were doing each time. If it is always the same file or the same step, that is useful to know.
4. Remove the app and install it again from the **App Store**. See [Remove an app](/apps/remove-apps).
5. If it started after a system update, see [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## Tell us

Press **Copy Details**, then paste them into the form at [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem). Say which app it was and what you were doing.

> Warning: The public copy of a report can be read by anyone. Look over the pasted details first and remove anything personal, such as file names you would not want others to see.

> Note: This window is new. It has been checked on screen, but not yet with every kind of crash, so on some PCs it may not appear.
