## Where a drive should appear

When you plug in a USB stick or external drive, a notification appears near the clock and the drive is listed in the sidebar of **Files**. It does not get a letter such as E: as it would on Windows. See [Where is the C drive](/windows/drive-letters).

If you missed the notification, open **Files** and look in the sidebar. Choose the drive to open it.

## Plain checks

1. Unplug the drive, wait five seconds and plug it in again.
2. Try a different USB socket. On a desktop PC, use one on the back.
3. Plug it straight into the PC, not through a hub or a dock.
4. For a drive with its own cable, try another cable.
5. A large external hard drive may need more power. Use its second USB plug or its power adapter if it has one.
6. Look for a light on the drive. No light and no sound usually means no power or a dead drive.
7. Try the drive in another computer. If it does not work there either, the drive is the problem.
8. Restart the PC with the drive unplugged, then plug it in once the desktop is up.

## The drive appears but will not open

- **It asks for a password.** The drive is encrypted. Enter the password it was set up with. A drive locked with Windows BitLocker may not open here.
- **It gives an error about being in use or not cleanly removed.** The drive was last used in Windows and not removed safely. Plug it into a Windows PC, use "Safely remove" there, and bring it back.
- **It opens but you cannot save to it.** The drive is read-only, or it was not removed safely last time. Some sticks and cards also have a small lock switch on the side.

## A new or blank drive

A brand new drive may need to be formatted before first use. OneClick OS includes an app called **Disks** for this.

> Warning: Formatting erases everything on the drive. Never format a drive to try to see the files on it. Check twice that you have chosen the right drive.

## A phone or camera

Phones do not appear as plain drives. Unlock the phone and choose file transfer on the phone's own screen when it asks what the USB connection is for. iPhones are more limited. See [iPhone, iTunes and iCloud](/windows/itunes-and-iphone).

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Most drives work, but some card readers and docks do not yet.

See [USB sticks and external drives](/desktop/external-drives) for normal use. If the drive works elsewhere and not here, please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).
