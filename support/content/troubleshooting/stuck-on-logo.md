title: Stuck on the OneClick logo
summary: Wait a few minutes, restart once, then start the earlier version.
order: 30
---
The OneClick logo is shown while the PC starts and while it shuts down. It normally clears within a minute.

## Wait first

The first start after installing, and the first start after an update, can take longer than usual. Give it five minutes before doing anything. Watch the disk light, if the PC has one. A flickering light means it is still working.

## Restart once

1. Hold the power button until the PC turns off.
2. Unplug USB sticks, external drives and anything else you do not need.
3. Wait ten seconds and turn the PC on.

A single hang is often gone after a restart.

## See what it is doing

Press **Esc** while the logo is showing. On many PCs this swaps the logo for the start-up messages. You do not need to understand them, but they tell you two things:

- If lines keep appearing, the PC is still working. Wait.
- If the same line stays for several minutes, take a photo of the screen. That photo is the most useful thing you can attach to a report.

## Start the earlier version

If it began after an update, go back to the version you had before.

1. Turn the PC on. Hold **Shift** or tap **Esc** as it starts, until the start-up list appears.
2. Choose the second entry in the list.
3. When the desktop is up, open **Settings**, choose **Update** and press **Undo the last update**.

See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## Stuck on the logo while shutting down

If the logo stays when you shut down or restart, wait two minutes. An app may be slow to close. If nothing changes, hold the power button until the PC turns off. Your saved files are not harmed by this.

## Still stuck

OneClick OS is an early test version and does not start on every PC yet. See [The PC will not start OneClick OS](/troubleshooting/wont-start), and please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of the PC.
