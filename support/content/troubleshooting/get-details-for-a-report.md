The report form asks for a few details. Here is where each one is. If the PC will not start, skip what you cannot get and say so.

## Your OneClick version

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Update**.
2. Read the line that says "You have OneClick OS" followed by a number.

Put that number in the report. The same version is shown in **About This PC**.

## Your computer's make and model

- **A laptop** has a label underneath, and often the model name printed near the keyboard or screen.
- **A bought desktop PC** has a label on the back or side.
- **A PC built from parts** has no model. Give the processor and graphics card instead.

For the parts inside:

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `about`.
2. Choose **About This PC**.
3. Note the processor, the memory and the graphics.

See [About This PC](/settings/about-this-pc).

## Which version you installed

Say whether you installed the standard version or the Nvidia version. See [Which version for Nvidia graphics](/getting-started/nvidia-version).

## Details from an app that closed

When an app closes unexpectedly, a window appears with a **Copy Details** button.

1. Press **Copy Details**. The button changes to **Copied**.
2. Go to the report form and paste into **What happened?** with **Ctrl+V**.

See [An app closed unexpectedly](/troubleshooting/app-crashed).

## A message on the screen

Type it out word for word, or take a picture.

- If the desktop is working, use **Screenshot** from the **Start menu**. See [Take a screenshot](/desktop/screenshots).
- If the PC will not start, take a photo of the screen with your phone.

The form takes text only. Describe what the picture shows, and keep the picture in case we ask for it.

## What happened and when

These are the most useful details of all, and they are not on the PC:

- What you did just before it went wrong.
- Whether it happens every time.
- Whether it used to work, and what changed. If you have just updated, say so.
- For hardware that does not work, such as Wi-Fi, a printer or a controller, its make and model.

## Check before you send

The public copy of a report can be read by anyone. Private details are removed automatically, but that can miss things. Read over what you pasted and remove names, addresses and anything else you would not want others to see. Never include a password.

Then send it at [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).
