OneClick OS is an early test version. Things will go wrong, and reports are how they get fixed. A report takes a few minutes and you do not need an account.

## Send a report

1. Go to [oneclickos.org/report](https://oneclickos.org/report) on any device. Your phone is fine if the PC will not start.
2. Choose what kind of problem it is:
- Something doesn't work
- It won't install or start
- A game or app won't run
- It looks wrong
- An idea or request
- A problem with this website
3. Fill in **What happened?**
4. Fill in **What did you expect to happen?**
5. Add your computer's make and model, if you know it.
6. Add your OneClick version. It is shown in **Settings**, then **Update**.
7. Add your email only if you want a reply.
8. Press **Send report**.

For where to find each of these, see [Find the details we ask for in a report](/troubleshooting/get-details-for-a-report).

## Write a report that can be fixed

- Say what you did, step by step, just before it went wrong.
- Copy any message on the screen word for word.
- Say whether it happens every time or only sometimes.
- Say whether it worked before, and what changed. For example, "it started after the last update".
- One problem per report.

If an app closed unexpectedly, press **Copy Details** in the window that appeared and paste that in. See [An app closed unexpectedly](/troubleshooting/app-crashed).

## Where your report goes

- **A public copy** is posted on OneClick's public issue list so other testers can see it. Your email is removed, along with anything that looks private, such as email addresses, phone numbers and keys.
- **The full report**, with your email if you gave one, goes only to a private place that Codecraft staff read. It is used to understand the problem and to reply to you.

> Warning: The removal of private details is automatic and can miss things. Do not put passwords or anything private in a report.

## What a report is not

Nothing is sent from your PC by itself. OneClick OS has no automatic crash reports and no usage tracking. A report exists only because you wrote and sent it. See [Privacy settings](/settings/privacy).

## While you wait

If the problem started after an update, you can go back to the version you had before. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).
