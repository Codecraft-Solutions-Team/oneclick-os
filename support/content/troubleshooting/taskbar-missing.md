title: The taskbar has disappeared
summary: The taskbar is probably hidden. Bring it back from the Taskbar page in Settings.
order: 130
---
The taskbar is almost certainly still there. The most likely cause is that it has been set to hide.

## Move the pointer to the edge

Push the pointer against the bottom edge of the screen and hold it there. If the taskbar slides into view, it is set to hide. Try the top edge as well, because the taskbar can be placed there.

## Check it is not behind a full-screen app

A game, a video or a web browser in full-screen mode covers the taskbar. Press **Esc** or **F11** to leave full screen.

## Get to the desktop without the taskbar

Move the pointer into the bottom-right corner of the screen and hold it there for about a second. All windows move out of the way and you see the desktop. This corner always works, even with the taskbar hidden. Do it again to bring the windows back.

Pressing the **Windows key** on the keyboard should open the Start menu.

## Turn hiding off

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Taskbar**. If you cannot reach the Start menu, press the **Windows key** and type `taskbar`.
2. Under **Behaviour**, choose **Always shown**.

The change applies straight away. The other two choices are **Hide automatically while an app covers it** and **Always hidden until the pointer reaches the edge**.

The same page sets **Position** (**Bottom** or **Top**) and **Size**. See [Change how the taskbar behaves](/desktop/taskbar-settings).

## Is it Mac style

In Mac style there is no taskbar along the bottom. There is a menu bar at the top and a floating dock in the middle of the bottom edge. If that is what you see and you want the taskbar back:

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Look**.
2. Choose **Windows style**.

See [Switch between Windows style and Mac style](/desktop/look-windows-or-mac).

## Still missing

1. Restart the PC.
2. If it vanished after an update, see [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).
3. Please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) and say what you see at the bottom of the screen.
