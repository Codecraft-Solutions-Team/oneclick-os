title: An app will not open
summary: Wait, check Task Manager, restart, then update or reinstall the app.
order: 80
---
You choose an app and nothing appears. Try these in order.

## Wait a moment

Some apps are slow the first time they open, especially large ones and just after installing. The pointer shows a busy sign and the taskbar shows the app is starting. Give it thirty seconds before choosing it again. Choosing it many times can make it slower.

## Check it is not already open

Look along the taskbar. The app may be open behind another window or on another desktop. See [Use more than one desktop](/desktop/virtual-desktops).

## Close a stuck copy

An app can be running without a window.

1. Open **Task Manager** from the **Start menu**.
2. Look for the app in the list.
3. Select it and end it.
4. Open the app again.

See [Task Manager](/desktop/task-manager).

## Restart the PC

A restart clears most problems of this kind.

## Is it still installing

Apps you chose in the welcome tour are installed in the background after you first sign in, with notifications showing progress. An app from that list will not open until its install has finished. This needs an internet connection.

## Update or reinstall the app

1. Open the **App Store** and look under **Updates**. Install any update for the app. See [Update your apps](/apps/update-apps).
2. If that does not help, remove the app and install it again. See [Remove an app](/apps/remove-apps) and [Install apps from the App Store](/apps/install-apps).

## Check the disk is not full

Apps can fail to open when there is no space left. See [The disk is full](/troubleshooting/out-of-space).

## Windows programs

A file ending in `.exe` or `.msi` is a Windows program. It opens through Bottles, and not every program works. See [The Windows Apps page](/settings/windows-apps-page).

For a game, see [A game will not start](/gaming/game-wont-start).

## Still not opening

If a window appears saying the app closed unexpectedly, see [An app closed unexpectedly](/troubleshooting/app-crashed). Otherwise, please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) and name the app.
