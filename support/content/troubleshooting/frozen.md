title: The screen is frozen
summary: Work out whether one app or the whole PC is stuck, and what to do for each.
order: 90
---
First find out how much is frozen. Move the mouse.

## The pointer moves, but one app does not respond

Only that app is stuck.

1. Wait up to a minute. A busy app often recovers, for example while saving a large file.
2. Press the close button on the app's window. If the app is not answering, you are asked whether to end it.
3. If that does not work, open **Task Manager** from the **Start menu**, select the app and end it.

Unsaved work in that app may be lost. Other apps are not affected. See [Task Manager](/desktop/task-manager).

## The pointer moves, but nothing can be clicked

A window may be waiting for an answer behind another window, or a game or video may have taken over the screen.

- Press **Esc**.
- Look along the taskbar for a window asking a question.
- Move the pointer into the bottom-right corner of the screen and hold it there for a second. This shows the desktop and clears the windows out of the way.

## Nothing moves at all

1. Wait two minutes. A PC that is short of memory can stop for a while and then come back.
2. Check that the mouse and keyboard are still connected. Try another USB socket. Check the batteries in a wireless one.
3. Press **Caps Lock**. If its light goes on and off, the PC is still running and the screen is the problem.
4. If nothing responds, hold the power button for about ten seconds until the PC turns off. Wait, then turn it on.

> Warning: Turning the PC off this way loses unsaved work. Use it only when nothing else responds. It does not damage the system. If the PC has trouble starting afterwards, see [The PC will not start OneClick OS](/troubleshooting/wont-start).

## If it keeps freezing

- Notice what was open each time. One app or game may be the cause.
- Close apps and browser tabs you are not using. See [The PC feels slow](/troubleshooting/slow).
- Check there is free disk space. See [The disk is full](/troubleshooting/out-of-space).
- If it began after an update, see [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version, and freezes can be caused by hardware that is not fully supported yet. Please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of the PC and what you were doing.
