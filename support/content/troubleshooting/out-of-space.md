title: The disk is full
summary: Empty Trash, clear Downloads, and remove apps and games you do not use.
order: 110
---
When the disk is full, apps can fail to open, files will not save and updates will not download. Clearing a few gigabytes fixes it.

## Quick wins

1. **Empty Trash.** Deleted files stay there and still take space. Open **Files**, find **Trash** in the sidebar and empty it.
2. **Clear Downloads.** Open **Files** and go to Downloads. Delete installers and anything you only needed once. Then empty Trash again.
3. **Remove apps and games you do not use.** See [Remove an app](/apps/remove-apps). Games are often the biggest things on the disk. Remove games from inside the launcher you installed them with, such as Steam.

## Find the big folders

Open **Disk Usage** from the **Start menu** and scan your Home folder. It shows which folders are largest. See [See what is using your disk space](/settings/storage).

Videos, phone backups and old copies of large files are the usual finds.

## Move files off the PC

Copy photos, videos and old documents to an external drive, then delete them from the PC once you have checked the copies open. See [USB sticks and external drives](/desktop/external-drives) and [Back up your files](/updates/back-up-your-files).

## What not to delete

Only delete things in your own Home folder. Do not delete system folders or folders you do not recognise, and do not go looking for system files to remove. The system manages its own space.

> Warning: Files removed from Trash cannot be brought back.

## Earlier system versions

Each update keeps the version from before, so you can undo it. This takes some space and is managed for you. There is no button to remove it, and you should not try to remove it by hand.

## The disk is small

If the disk fills again within days, it is too small for what you keep on it. Keep large files on an external drive.

If you share the disk with Windows, the space OneClick OS has is the part you gave it during install. See [Keep Windows and install OneClick beside it](/getting-started/dual-boot-with-windows).

If you cannot free any space, or the PC will not start because the disk is full, please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem).
