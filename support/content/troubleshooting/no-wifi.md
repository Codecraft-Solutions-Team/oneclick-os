title: Wi-Fi is missing or will not connect
summary: Work out whether the problem is the network, the password or the PC's Wi-Fi part.
order: 40
---
First work out which problem you have. Choose the network icon beside the clock and look at the list.

## Your network is listed but will not connect

1. Type the password again. Capital letters matter, and `0` and `O` are easy to mix up.
2. Check that another device, such as your phone, can use the same Wi-Fi.
3. Turn the router off at the wall, wait thirty seconds and turn it on. Give it two minutes.
4. Remove the saved network and join it again. Open the **Start menu**, type `wifi`, choose **Wi-Fi & Network**, select the network and remove it. Then connect as new.
5. Move closer to the router and try once more.

A hotel, school or cafe network may need you to open a web browser and agree to a page before the internet works.

## Other networks are listed but not yours

- Restart the router.
- Stand next to it and look again.
- If your router has two network names, try the other one.

## No networks at all, or no Wi-Fi option

1. Check the Wi-Fi switch or key on the laptop. Check that aeroplane mode is off.
2. Restart the PC.
3. If you also have Windows on this PC, shut Windows down fully before starting OneClick OS. Use Restart in Windows, not Shut down, then pick OneClick OS. Windows can keep hold of the Wi-Fi part when it only half shuts down.

If there is still nothing, the PC's Wi-Fi part is probably not supported yet.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Most Wi-Fi works without setup, but some models do not work at all yet. There is no driver to download from the maker's website.

## Get online another way for now

- Plug in a network cable. See [Wired internet and VPN](/settings/wired-and-vpn).
- Connect your phone by USB cable and turn on USB tethering in the phone's hotspot settings.
- Use a small USB Wi-Fi adapter. Not every model works, so ask before buying.

## It stopped after an update

Go back to the earlier version. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

Then please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of the PC. See [Connect to Wi-Fi](/settings/wifi) for the normal steps.
