title: The screen is the wrong size or blurry
summary: Set the screen's own resolution, then use scale to change the size of things.
order: 60
---
There are two separate settings, and mixing them up is the usual cause.

- **Resolution** is how many dots the screen shows. The screen's own resolution is the sharpest.
- **Scale** is how big things are drawn.

A blurry or stretched picture means the resolution is wrong. Things being too small or too large means the scale is wrong.

## Set the resolution

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `display`.
2. Choose **Display & Monitor**.
3. Pick the screen.
4. Choose the highest resolution in the list. That is normally the screen's own.
5. Apply. Keep the change if the picture looks right. If it looks wrong, wait and it goes back.

## Set the scale

On the same page, change the scale. Raise it if everything is too small, lower it if everything is too big. See [Make text and everything bigger](/settings/text-size).

## One app is blurry and the rest are sharp

Some apps, mostly older ones and Windows programs, are drawn at normal size and then stretched when the scale is above 100%. Close the app and open it again after changing the scale. If it stays blurry, that is a limit of the app.

## The picture goes off the edge, or has black bars

This is common on a television. Look in the television's own picture menu for a setting called something like "Just scan", "Screen fit" or "1:1" and turn it on.

## The right resolution is not in the list

- Try a different cable or socket. An old or damaged cable can hide the higher resolutions.
- Plug the monitor straight into the PC, without an adapter or dock.
- Restart with the monitor connected and on.

If the only choice is a low resolution such as 1024x768, the graphics card is not being used properly. On a PC with Nvidia graphics, check you have the Nvidia version. See [Which version for Nvidia graphics](/getting-started/nvidia-version).

## It changed after an update

See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).
