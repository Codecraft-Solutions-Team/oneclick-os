title: The PC will not start OneClick OS
summary: Plain checks first, then the earlier version from the start-up list.
order: 10
---
Work through these in order. Stop as soon as the PC starts.

## Plain checks first

1. Hold the power button until the PC turns off. Wait ten seconds and turn it on again.
2. Unplug everything except the screen, keyboard and mouse. That includes USB sticks, external drives and the installer USB stick. A PC can try to start from one of those.
3. On a laptop, plug in the charger.
4. Check that the screen is on and set to the right input.

## It started fine until an update

Every update keeps the version you had before. You can start that one.

1. Turn the PC on and watch for the start-up list. If it does not appear, hold **Shift** or tap **Esc** as the PC starts.
2. Choose the second entry in the list. That is the version from before the update.
3. Once the desktop is up, open **Settings**, choose **Update** and press **Undo the last update**, so it stays on that version.

Your files, apps and settings are not touched. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## The PC starts something else

- If it goes straight to Windows, see [Choose Windows or OneClick at start-up](/getting-started/choose-at-startup).
- If it shows a message such as "no bootable device", the PC is not looking at the disk OneClick OS is on. The PC's own start-up settings choose which disk is used. See [Start your PC from the USB stick](/getting-started/start-from-usb) for how to open them, and pick the internal disk.

## It has never started since installing

The install may not have finished, or this PC may not be supported yet. OneClick OS is an early test version and does not run on every PC. See [Will it run on my PC](/getting-started/is-my-pc-supported).

> Warning: Do not reinstall as a first step. Reinstalling can erase your files, and it does not help if the PC itself is the problem.

## Still stuck

If you see a black screen, see [Black screen after starting](/troubleshooting/black-screen). If the logo stays forever, see [Stuck on the OneClick logo](/troubleshooting/stuck-on-logo). Otherwise, please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) from another device and say exactly what is on the screen.
