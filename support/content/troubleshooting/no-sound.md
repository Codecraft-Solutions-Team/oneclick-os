title: No sound
summary: Check the volume, then which speaker is chosen, then the app itself.
order: 50
---
Most sound problems are a muted volume or the wrong speaker being selected.

## Check the volume

1. Choose the speaker icon beside the clock.
2. Make sure the volume is up and not muted.
3. The same panel lists apps that are playing. Check that the app is not muted by itself.
4. Check the volume knob or button on your speakers, headphones or monitor.

## Check which speaker is chosen

The PC may be sending sound somewhere you are not listening, such as a monitor with no speakers or headphones that are in a drawer.

1. Open the **Start menu** and type `sound`.
2. Choose **Sound**.
3. Look at the output devices and pick the one you want.
4. Play something. Try each output in turn if you are not sure which is which.

This happens most often after plugging in a monitor, a dock or a Bluetooth device.

## Check the cable and the app

- Push the headphone or speaker plug fully in. On a desktop PC, the green socket is the usual one.
- Play a different sound, for example a video in the web browser. If that works, the problem is in the first app. Look at the app's own sound settings.
- In a web browser, a tab can be muted on its own. Right-click the tab to check.

## Restart

Restart the PC. This fixes sound that stopped after sleep or after unplugging a device.

## Bluetooth headphones

If they are connected but silent, choose them as the output on the Sound page. If they will not connect, see [Bluetooth will not connect](/troubleshooting/bluetooth-not-working).

## It stopped after an update

Go back to the version you had before. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## Still nothing

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. On some PCs, mostly certain recent laptops, the built-in speakers or microphone are not supported yet. Headphones on a USB or Bluetooth connection often work when the built-in speakers do not.

Please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of the PC. See [Sound, speakers and microphones](/settings/sound) for the normal settings.
