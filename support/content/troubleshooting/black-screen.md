summary: Check the screen and cable, then start the earlier version from the start-up list.

A black screen after the PC starts is nearly always about the screen or the graphics card, not your files. Your files are safe.

## Check the screen first

1. Make sure the monitor is switched on and set to the right input.
2. Push the cable in at both ends. Try another cable or another socket if you have one.
3. On a desktop PC with a separate graphics card, plug the monitor into the card, not into the sockets higher up on the back of the PC.
4. If two screens are connected, unplug one.
5. Move the mouse and press a key. The PC may only be asleep.

## Restart

Hold the power button until the PC turns off. Wait ten seconds and turn it on again. A single black screen after sleep or a restart often does not come back.

## If it began after an update

1. Turn the PC on. Hold **Shift** or tap **Esc** as it starts, so the start-up list appears.
2. Choose the second entry. That is the version from before the update.
3. When the desktop appears, open **Settings**, choose **Update** and press **Undo the last update**.

See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## Black screen after waking from sleep

Sleep does not work properly on every PC yet. Hold the power button to turn the PC off, start it again, and set it to never sleep for now. See [Power, sleep and battery](/settings/power-and-battery).

## Nvidia graphics

PCs with Nvidia graphics need the Nvidia version of OneClick OS. With the standard version, a black screen is a common result. See [Which version for Nvidia graphics](/getting-started/nvidia-version).

## A black screen with a pointer

If you can see the mouse pointer and nothing else, the desktop did not finish loading. Wait a full minute, then restart. If it happens every time, start the earlier version as described above.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version and some graphics hardware is not supported yet. Please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of the PC and its graphics card.
