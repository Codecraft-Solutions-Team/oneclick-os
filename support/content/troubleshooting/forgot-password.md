OneClick OS has no online account, so there is no "email me a reset link". Your password exists only on this PC. Here is what you can do.

## Check the plain things first

- **Caps Lock.** Check the light on the keyboard.
- **Num Lock.** If the password has numbers and you use the number pad, check it is on.
- **Keyboard layout.** If the layout on the sign-in screen is not the one you normally type with, some keys give different characters. Symbols such as `@`, `"` and `#` are the usual ones.
- **The right account.** If there is more than one person on the PC, check your name is the one selected.
- **Older passwords.** Try the one you used before, if you changed it recently.

Type slowly. If the sign-in screen has a way to show the password as you type, use it.

## Ask another administrator on this PC

If someone else has an administrator account on the same PC, they can set a new password for you.

1. They sign in to their own account.
2. They open the **Start menu**, type `users` and choose **Users**.
3. They select your account and set a new password, confirming with their own.
4. You sign in with the new password, then change it to one only you know. See [Change your password](/settings/password).

Your files are not affected. Passwords the PC had saved for you, such as Wi-Fi and app passwords, may need to be entered again.

## If there is no other administrator

We have to be honest here. OneClick OS does not have a built-in password reset screen that we can point you to, and we will not give you steps we have not tested.

Please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) from another device and leave your email so we can reply. Say that you are locked out and whether the disk was encrypted when you installed.

> Warning: Do not reinstall to get back in unless you accept losing what is on the PC. Reinstalling can erase your files.

> Note: If you turned on disk encryption, that has its own password, asked for when the PC starts. Nobody can recover that one, including us. See [Protect your files if the PC is lost](/updates/disk-encryption).

## So it does not happen again

- Write the password down and keep it somewhere safe at home.
- Add a second administrator account for someone you trust. See [Add another person to this PC](/settings/users).
- Keep a backup of your files. See [Back up your files](/updates/back-up-your-files).
