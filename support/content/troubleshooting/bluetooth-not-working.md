title: Bluetooth will not connect
summary: Check pairing mode and batteries, then remove the device and pair it again.

## Check the device

1. Charge it or change its batteries. Low power is the most common cause.
2. Put it into pairing mode. Being switched on is not enough. Usually you hold a button until a light flashes. The manual says how.
3. Bring it within a metre of the PC.
4. Turn Bluetooth off on your phone or tablet for a moment. Many headphones connect to the last device they used and ignore the PC.

## Check the PC

1. Open the **Start menu**, type `bluetooth` and choose **Bluetooth**.
2. Make sure Bluetooth is turned on.
3. Check aeroplane mode is off.
4. Turn Bluetooth off and on again, then look for the device.

## Remove the device and pair again

If the device was paired before and now will not connect:

1. On the Bluetooth page, select the device and remove it.
2. Put the device back into pairing mode.
3. Add it again. See [Bluetooth devices](/settings/bluetooth).

Then restart the PC if it still fails.

## Connected, but no sound

Headphones can be connected without being chosen for sound. Open the Sound page and pick them as the output. See [No sound](/troubleshooting/no-sound).

## Keeps dropping out

- Move closer and take anything metal out from between the device and the PC.
- On a desktop PC, move a USB Bluetooth adapter to a socket on the front. A short USB extension cable helps.
- Keep the adapter away from USB 3 drives and hubs, which can interfere.

## There is no Bluetooth page, or it says no adapter

The PC either has no Bluetooth or its Bluetooth part is not supported yet. Many desktop PCs have none built in.

If you also have Windows on this PC, restart from Windows into OneClick OS instead of shutting down and starting cold, then check again.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Most Bluetooth adapters work, but not all. Some devices pair on Windows and not here yet. There is no driver to download.

## It stopped after an update

See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

If nothing helps, please [report the problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) with the make and model of both the PC and the device. For game controllers, see [Controllers](/gaming/controllers).
