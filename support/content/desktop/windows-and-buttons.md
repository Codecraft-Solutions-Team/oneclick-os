Windows on OneClick OS work the way you are used to. Each has a title bar along the top with three buttons.

## The three buttons

In Windows style the buttons are at the top right of the window, in this order:

- **Minimise.** Hides the window. It shrinks down towards the taskbar. Click its taskbar icon to bring it back.
- **Maximise.** Makes the window fill the screen. Click it again to go back to the earlier size.
- **Close.** Closes the window.

In Mac style the same three buttons are round and sit at the top left. The order there is close, minimise, maximise. See [Using Mac style](/desktop/mac-style).

## Move a window

Drag the title bar, which is the strip along the top of the window with its name on it.

## Resize a window

Move the pointer to any edge or corner of the window until it changes shape, then drag.

To fill the screen quickly, double-click the title bar. Double-click it again to go back.

## Switch between windows

- Click the app's icon on the taskbar.
- Hold **Alt** and tap **Tab** to step through your open windows. Let go of Alt on the one you want.

## Close a window from the keyboard

Press **Alt+F4** to close the window you are using.

## If a window will not close

If an app has stopped responding and its close button does nothing, see [The screen is frozen](/troubleshooting/frozen) or end the app from [Task Manager](/desktop/task-manager).
