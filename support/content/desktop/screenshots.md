OneClick OS includes an app called **Screenshot**. It takes a picture of your screen that you can save or paste somewhere else.

## Take one

1. Press the **Print Screen** key on your keyboard. On many keyboards it is labelled **PrtSc**. You can also open the **Start** menu and choose **Screenshot**, which is pinned there to begin with.
2. The Screenshot window opens. Choose what to capture:
   - the whole screen
   - one window
   - a rectangle that you drag out with the mouse
3. Take the capture. A preview appears.
4. Save it, or copy it so you can paste it into an email, a chat or a document.

> Note: On some laptops you need to hold the **Fn** key while pressing Print Screen.

## Where screenshots are saved

Saved screenshots go into your **Pictures** folder, usually in a folder of their own inside it, unless you choose somewhere else when saving. Open **Files** and look in **Pictures**. The Screenshot app's own settings let you change the folder.

## Mark up a screenshot

After a capture, the Screenshot window has drawing tools. You can add arrows, boxes and text, or hide part of the picture before you save it. This is useful for pointing at the exact thing that went wrong.

## Sending a screenshot with a problem report

A screenshot often explains a problem faster than words. Before you send one, check that it does not show anything private, such as a password, an email address or a bank page.

See [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) for where to send it.
