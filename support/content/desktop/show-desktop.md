When the screen is full of windows, you can clear them all with one action. They are not closed. Do the same thing again and they come back.

## Three ways to do it

### Click the end of the taskbar

In Windows style, the very end of the taskbar, to the right of the clock, is the show desktop strip. Click it to hide your windows. Click it again to bring them back.

### Use the bottom-right corner

Move the pointer into the bottom-right corner of the screen and leave it there for about a second. Your windows move away and the desktop appears.

This corner works even when the taskbar is hidden. It is there as a safety net, so you can always get back to a screen where the taskbar is visible.

> Note: The one-second wait is deliberate. It stops the desktop appearing every time the pointer passes through the corner.

### Use the keyboard

Hold the Windows key and press **D**. Press it again to bring your windows back.

## Hide only one window

To put away only the window you are using, press its minimise button, or click its icon on the taskbar. See [Move, resize and close windows](/desktop/windows-and-buttons).

## If the taskbar is not there

If you reach the desktop and still cannot see the taskbar, see [The taskbar has disappeared](/troubleshooting/taskbar-missing). To change when the taskbar hides, see [Change how the taskbar behaves](/desktop/taskbar-settings).
