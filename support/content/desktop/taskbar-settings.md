The **Taskbar** page in Settings controls when the taskbar shows, where it sits and how big it is.

## Open the Taskbar page

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Taskbar**. You can also type `taskbar` in the Start menu search.

The top of the page shows a small drawing of a desktop. It changes as you change the options, so you can see the result before you look at the real thing.

> Note: Changes apply immediately. There is no Apply button to press.

## The options

### Behaviour

- **Always shown.** The taskbar stays on screen all the time.
- **Hide automatically while an app covers it.** The taskbar stays visible until a window needs the space, then gets out of the way.
- **Always hidden until the pointer reaches the edge.** The taskbar stays out of sight until you move the pointer to its edge of the screen.

### Appearance

- **Floating (rounded bar with a gap around it).** Tick this for a rounded bar that sits slightly away from the screen edge. Untick it for a bar that runs edge to edge.

### Position

- **Bottom** or **Top**.

### Size

- **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**.

## If you hide the taskbar and cannot find it

Move the pointer right to the edge of the screen where the taskbar lives. If that does not work, rest the pointer in the bottom-right corner of the screen for about a second. Your windows move away and the desktop appears with the taskbar visible.

More help is in [The taskbar has disappeared](/troubleshooting/taskbar-missing).

## In Mac style

In Mac style this page changes the dock at the bottom of the screen, and the menu bar at the top is left alone. See [Using Mac style](/desktop/mac-style).

> Note: This page is new in the test version. If an option does nothing, please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report).
