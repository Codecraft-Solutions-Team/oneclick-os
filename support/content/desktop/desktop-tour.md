When you sign in you see a wallpaper with one bar along the bottom of the screen. That bar is the taskbar. Almost everything starts from it.

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. Some parts described here are new and may not be finished.

## What is on screen

Reading the taskbar from left to right:

- **Start button.** Opens the Start menu, where you find your apps, search and the power buttons. See [The Start menu](/desktop/start-menu).
- **Pinned apps.** To begin with these are your web browser, **Files**, **App Store**, **Settings** and **Update**. Click one to open it.
- **Open apps.** Every app you open shows its icon here. Click an icon to switch to that app.
- **The corner.** Small icons for things like Wi-Fi and sound, then the clock. See [The clock and icons in the corner](/desktop/system-tray).
- **Show desktop.** The very end of the taskbar hides your windows so you can see the desktop.

## How it behaves

- Double-click a file or folder to open it. A single click selects it. This is the same as Windows.
- Windows have their minimise, maximise and close buttons at the top right.
- Notifications appear at the bottom right.
- If you rest the pointer in the bottom-right corner of the screen for about a second, your windows move out of the way and the desktop appears.
