OneClick OS can arrange the desktop in two ways. You can switch whenever you like, and nothing is lost from your files or apps.

## The two styles

| | Windows style | Mac style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main bar | Taskbar along the bottom with a Start button | Menu bar along the top |
| Your apps | On the taskbar | In a centred, floating dock at the bottom |
| Window buttons | Top right | Top left |
| Notifications | Bottom right | Top right |
| Surfaces | Solid | See-through, like frosted glass |

Windows style is what you get on a new install.

## Switch style

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Look**. You can also type `look` in the Start menu search.
3. Under **Choose how your desktop works** you see two cards with a small picture of each layout: **Windows style** and **Mac style**.
4. Click the one you want.

The card says **Applying...** for a moment while the desktop rearranges itself. The card you are using is marked **Active**.

## What changes and what does not

Switching changes the layout: the bars, the dock, where the window buttons are, and how windows shrink when you minimise them.

It does not change your files, your installed apps, or your settings for things like Wi-Fi and sound.

> Note: Switching rebuilds the taskbar or dock from scratch. Apps you pinned there, and changes you made on the Taskbar page, may go back to the starting set. Your wallpaper may also go back to the OneClick one. Check them after switching.

## Fine-tune afterwards

After picking a style you can still change the size of the taskbar or dock and when it hides. Open **Settings** and choose **Taskbar**. See [Change how the taskbar behaves](/desktop/taskbar-settings).

For what is different day to day in Mac style, see [Using Mac style](/desktop/mac-style).

> Note: Mac style is new in this test version. If something looks wrong after switching, switch back to Windows style and please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report).
