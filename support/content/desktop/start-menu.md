The Start menu is where you open apps, search the PC and shut down. It is laid out much like the one in Windows 11.

## Open it

Click the **Start** button at the left end of the taskbar, or press the Windows key on your keyboard. Press **Esc** to close it.

> Note: If the Windows key does not open the menu on your PC, click the Start button and please [report it](https://oneclickos.org/report). This is an early test version.

## What is in it

From top to bottom:

- **Search box.** It says **Search apps, settings and files**. Type to search. See [Search for apps, files and settings](/desktop/search).
- **Pinned.** A grid of your pinned apps. Click one to open it.
- **All apps.** Press the **All apps** button to see every installed app in a list from A to Z. Press **Back** to return to your pinned apps.
- **Your name and the power buttons.** At the bottom you see your account name and four buttons: **Lock**, **Sign out**, **Restart** and **Shut down**. See [Lock, sign out, restart and shut down](/desktop/lock-sign-out-shut-down).

## What is pinned to begin with

A new account starts with these pinned: your web browser, **Files**, **App Store**, **Settings**, **Text Editor**, **Terminal**, **Screenshot**, **Update**, **Task Manager**, **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **GeForce NOW** and **Xbox Cloud Gaming**.

The last four are shortcuts that open a website in your browser. Nothing is installed for them, and you can unpin any you do not want.

## Change what is pinned

Right-click any app in the menu. You get two choices:

- **Pin to Start** or **Unpin from Start**
- **Add to taskbar**

More detail is in [Pin apps to Start and the taskbar](/desktop/pin-apps).
