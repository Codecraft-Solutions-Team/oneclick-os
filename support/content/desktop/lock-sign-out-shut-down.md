The power buttons are at the bottom of the Start menu, on the right, next to your name.

## The four buttons

Open the **Start** menu. Hold the pointer over a button to see its name.

- **Lock.** Hides your work behind the lock screen. Your apps stay open. Enter your password to carry on. Use this when you step away.
- **Sign out.** Closes your apps and returns to the sign-in screen, so someone else can use the PC with their own account.
- **Restart.** Closes everything and starts the PC again.
- **Shut down.** Closes everything and turns the PC off.

**Lock** happens straight away. **Sign out**, **Restart** and **Shut down** first show a screen asking you to confirm, with a short countdown. You can cancel there if you clicked by mistake.

> Warning: Save your work before you sign out, restart or shut down. Apps will be closed.

## From the keyboard

Hold the Windows key and press **L** to lock the screen.

## Sleep

There is no Sleep button in the Start menu at the moment. On a laptop, closing the lid normally puts it to sleep. To choose what the lid and the power button do, and when the PC sleeps by itself, see [Power, sleep and battery](/settings/power-and-battery).

## Restarting does not install anything by surprise

OneClick OS never adds an update to a restart or shut down unless you downloaded that update yourself. If you did press **Download** on the Update page, the new version is used the next time you restart. See [Download now, restart later](/updates/update-and-restart).
