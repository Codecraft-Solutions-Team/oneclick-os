You do not need to remember where something lives. Open the Start menu and type its name.

## Search from the Start menu

1. Click **Start** or press the Windows key.
2. Start typing. If nothing appears as you type, click the box that says **Search apps, settings and files** first.
3. Results appear under the box. Press **Enter** to open the first one, or click the one you want.

You can also press the **Down arrow** to move into the list, then **Enter** to open the highlighted result.

If there is no match you see **Nothing found for** followed by what you typed. Check the spelling or try a shorter word.

## What it finds

- **Settings pages.** These come first and say **Settings** under the name. For example, typing `display` shows **Display & Monitor**, and typing `wifi` shows **Wi-Fi & Network**. Choosing one opens Settings on that page.
- **Apps.** Any installed app, by name.
- **Files and more.** Files in your folders, and quick answers such as a sum you type in.

> Note: Settings and apps appear straight away. Files can take a few seconds longer, especially on a slower PC.

## Other ways to find things

- To browse every setting, see [Open Settings and find a setting](/settings/open-settings).
- To look through your folders, open **Files**. It has its own search for the folder you are in. See [Find and manage your files](/desktop/files).
- If you know the Windows name of something but not the OneClick name, see [Where things went](/windows/windows-to-oneclick).
