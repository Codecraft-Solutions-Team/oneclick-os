A virtual desktop is an extra, empty desktop on the same screen. You might keep email on one and a game on another, then switch between them instead of juggling windows. Windows 10 and 11 have the same idea under Task View.

OneClick OS starts with one desktop. You add more when you want them.

## Add a desktop

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Search for `virtual desktops` and open that page.
3. Add as many desktops as you want. You can name them.

You can also add one from the overview. Hold the Windows key and press **W**. All your open windows spread out, and your desktops are shown along the edge of the screen with a button to add another.

## Switch between desktops

- Hold the Windows key and press **W**, then click the desktop you want.
- Or hold **Ctrl** and the Windows key, then press the **Left arrow** or **Right arrow**.

## Move a window to another desktop

1. Hold the Windows key and press **W**.
2. Drag the window onto the desktop you want it on.

You can also right-click a window's title bar and look for the option to move it to another desktop.

## Good to know

- The taskbar, Start menu and wallpaper are the same on every desktop.
- Apps keep running on a desktop you are not looking at.
- The taskbar may show only the apps on the desktop you are looking at. If an app seems to be missing, check your other desktops.
- You can remove a desktop the same way you added it. Its windows move to another desktop. They do not close.
