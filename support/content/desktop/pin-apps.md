Pinning keeps an app where you can reach it quickly. You can pin to the Start menu, to the taskbar, or both.

## Pin to the Start menu

1. Open the **Start** menu.
2. Find the app. Press **All apps** to see the full list, or type its name in the search box.
3. Right-click the app and choose **Pin to Start**.

The app now appears in the **Pinned** grid.

To remove it, right-click it and choose **Unpin from Start**. This only removes the shortcut. The app stays installed.

## Add an app to the taskbar

1. Open the **Start** menu and find the app.
2. Right-click it and choose **Add to taskbar**.

The app's icon appears on the taskbar and stays there, whether the app is open or not.

You can also do this from the taskbar itself. Open the app, right-click its icon on the taskbar and choose the option to pin it. Use the same right-click menu to unpin it later.

> Note: The small right-click menu in Start may appear at the bottom-left of the Start menu instead of beside the app you clicked. This is a known issue in the test version. The menu still works.

## In Mac style

If you use Mac style, **Add to taskbar** adds the app to the dock. On the dock itself, right-click an icon and choose **Pin to dock** or **Unpin from dock**. See [Using Mac style](/desktop/mac-style).

> Note: Switching between Windows style and Mac style rebuilds the taskbar or dock. Apps you pinned there may go back to the starting set, and you may need to pin them again.
