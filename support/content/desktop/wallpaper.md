The wallpaper is the picture behind your windows. OneClick OS starts with its own dark blue wallpaper. You can replace it with any picture you like.

## Change it from the desktop

1. Go to the desktop. If windows are in the way, see [Get back to the desktop quickly](/desktop/show-desktop).
2. Right-click an empty part of the desktop.
3. Choose the option for the desktop and wallpaper.
4. Pick one of the pictures shown, or use the button to add your own picture and choose a file, for example from your **Pictures** folder.
5. Apply the change.

## Change it from a picture

In **Files**, right-click a picture. If the menu offers to set it as the wallpaper, choose that.

You can also get there through **Settings**. Search for `wallpaper`.

## Tips for a good result

- Use a picture at least as large as your screen. A small picture stretched to fit looks blurry.
- The wallpaper window lets you choose how the picture is placed, such as scaled to fill the screen or centred.
- The same window can show a plain colour or a slideshow of a folder of pictures instead of a single image.

## What the wallpaper does not change

- The lock screen and the sign-in screen have their own picture. Changing the desktop wallpaper does not change them.
- Switching between Windows style and Mac style may put the OneClick wallpaper back. If that happens, set your picture again.

## Go back to the OneClick wallpaper

Open the wallpaper window again and choose the OneClick picture from the ones shown.
