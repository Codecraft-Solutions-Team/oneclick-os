At the right-hand end of the taskbar there is a group of small icons, then the clock. In Windows this area is called the system tray. It works the same way here.

## The small icons

Each icon is a quick way to check or change one thing. Which icons you see depends on your PC, but these are the usual ones:

- **Network.** Shows whether you are connected. Click it to pick a Wi-Fi network. See [Connect to Wi-Fi](/settings/wifi).
- **Sound.** Click it to change the volume or choose between speakers and headphones. See [Sound, speakers and microphones](/settings/sound).
- **Bluetooth.** Appears if your PC has Bluetooth.
- **Battery.** Appears on laptops and shows how much charge is left.
- **Notifications.** Shows messages you may have missed. See [Notifications](/desktop/notifications).

Click an icon to open a small panel for it. Click anywhere else to close the panel.

## Hidden icons

Icons that are not needed right now are tucked away to save space. Click the small arrow beside the icons to see the rest.

Some apps also put their own icon here while they are running.

## The clock

The clock shows the time and usually the date. Click it to see a calendar.

If the time is wrong, see [Date, time and time zone](/settings/date-and-time). If it is only wrong after you have used Windows on the same PC, see [The clock is wrong after using Windows](/troubleshooting/clock-wrong-after-windows).

## The strip at the very end

To the right of the clock is the show desktop strip. Click it to hide all your windows. See [Get back to the desktop quickly](/desktop/show-desktop).

## In Mac style

In Mac style the icons and the clock sit at the right-hand end of the menu bar at the top of the screen.
