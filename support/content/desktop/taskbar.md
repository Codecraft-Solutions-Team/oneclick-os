The taskbar is the bar along the bottom of the screen. It works like the Windows taskbar.

## What is on it

From left to right:

1. The **Start** button. See [The Start menu](/desktop/start-menu).
2. Your pinned apps. A new account has your web browser, **Files**, **App Store**, **Settings** and **Update**.
3. Any other apps that are open.
4. The small icons and the clock. See [The clock and icons in the corner](/desktop/system-tray).
5. The show desktop strip at the very end. See [Get back to the desktop quickly](/desktop/show-desktop).

## Use it

- **Open an app.** Click its icon.
- **Switch apps.** Click the icon of an app that is already open. A marker on the icon shows which apps are running.
- **Minimise an app.** Click the icon of the app you are looking at. Click it again to bring it back.
- **Close an app or see more options.** Right-click its icon.
- **Pin an app.** See [Pin apps to Start and the taskbar](/desktop/pin-apps).

When you open an app, its taskbar icon shows that it is starting, so you know your click worked.

## Change it

You can hide the taskbar, make it float, move it to the top of the screen, or make it smaller or larger. See [Change how the taskbar behaves](/desktop/taskbar-settings).

If you prefer a menu bar at the top and a dock at the bottom, see [Switch between Windows style and Mac style](/desktop/look-windows-or-mac).

## If it disappears

If the taskbar is set to hide, move the pointer to the edge of the screen where it lives. You can also rest the pointer in the bottom-right corner of the screen for about a second to show the desktop, where the taskbar is visible.

If it is still missing, see [The taskbar has disappeared](/troubleshooting/taskbar-missing).
