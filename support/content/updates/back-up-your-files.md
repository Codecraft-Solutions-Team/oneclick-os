A backup is a second copy of your files kept somewhere else. If the PC is lost, stolen or its disk fails, the backup is what you get back.

> Warning: Undoing an update is not a backup. System updates and **Undo the last update** only replace the system. They never touch your files, which also means they cannot bring back a file you deleted or lost.

## What to back up

Everything that is yours is in your **Home** folder. The folders that matter most are usually:

- **Documents**
- **Pictures**
- **Videos**
- **Music**
- **Downloads**, if you keep things there
- **Desktop**

You do not need to back up apps. They can be installed again from the App Store.

## Copy to an external drive

This is the most direct way.

1. Plug in a USB stick or external drive with enough free space.
2. Open **Files**.
3. Select the folders you want to keep.
4. Copy them, open the drive from the left side of Files, and paste.
5. Wait for the copy to finish, then eject the drive before unplugging it. See [USB sticks and external drives](/desktop/external-drives).

Keep the drive somewhere other than next to the PC. Repeat the copy regularly. A backup from last year only brings back last year's files.

## Keep files in a cloud drive

If you use OneDrive or Google Drive, you can connect it so it appears in **Files**, then keep important folders there. See [Connect OneDrive](/apps/onedrive) and [Connect Google Drive](/apps/google-drive).

> Note: Cloud drive support is new in this test version and may not be finished. Do not make it your only copy yet.

## How to tell a backup is good

- Open a few files from the backup itself, not from the PC, to check they work.
- Have at least two copies of anything you could not replace, in two different places.

## There is no automatic backup yet

OneClick OS does not yet include its own tool that backs up on a schedule. For now, copying is something you do yourself.
