title: Undo an update
summary: Go back to the version you had before, with your files untouched.
order: 20
---
Every system update keeps the version you had before on your PC. An update replaces only the system, never your personal files, apps, or settings. Going back needs no download and takes one restart.

## Go back from Settings

1. Open **Settings** from the Start menu and choose **Update**, or open **Update** directly from the Start menu.
2. Scroll down and press **Undo the last update**.
3. Enter your password when asked, then restart when prompted.

When the PC starts again, it is back on the exact system version you had before the last update. Your documents, photos, apps, and settings stay as they were.

## If the computer will not start

1. Turn the PC on and watch for the start-up list. On a PC with only OneClick OS, that list is normally hidden, so hold **Shift** or tap **Esc** as the PC turns on.
2. Choose the second entry in the list. That is the system version from before the update.
3. Once the desktop is up, open **Settings**, choose **Update**, and press **Undo the last update** so the PC stays on that version every time you start it.

> Note: **Undo the last update** steps back one update with no download. If you want to pick a different earlier version by its number instead, see [Go back to an earlier version](/updates/previous-versions).

## Still stuck?

If your PC still will not start after choosing the earlier entry, see [The PC will not start OneClick OS](/troubleshooting/wont-start) and [An update will not download or finish](/updates/update-failed). Please also [report the problem](/reports) from another device so the update can be fixed for everyone.
