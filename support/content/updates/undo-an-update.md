title: Undo an update
summary: Go back to the version you had before, with your files untouched.
order: 20
---
An update replaces the system, never your files. The version you had before is kept, so going back takes one restart.

## Go back from Settings

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Update**.
2. Press **Undo the last update**.
3. Enter your password and restart when asked.

Your documents, apps and settings stay as they are.

## If the computer will not start

1. Turn the computer on and watch for the start-up list. If it does not appear, hold **Shift** or tap **Esc** as the computer starts.
2. Choose the second entry in the list. That is the version from before the update.
3. Once the desktop is up, open **Settings**, choose **Update**, and press **Undo the last update** so it stays on that version.

> Note: Please [report the problem](https://oneclickos.org/report) so the update can be fixed for everyone.
