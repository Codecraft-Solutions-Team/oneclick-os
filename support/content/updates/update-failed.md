title: An update will not download or finish
summary: What to try when the Update page says something went wrong.
order: 60
---
When a check or a download fails, the **Update** page shows **Something went wrong**, usually with a short reason underneath. Your PC is not harmed. A failed download changes nothing, and you stay on the version you have.

## Try these first

1. **Check the internet.** Open a website in your browser. If it does not load, fix the connection first. See [Wi-Fi is missing or will not connect](/troubleshooting/no-wifi).
2. **Try again.** Press **Check now**, then **Download**. A download that was cut off can be started again.
3. **Enter your password when asked.** A download needs your password. If you close the password box or type the wrong password, the download stops.
4. **Check free space.** An update needs room to download. See [The disk is full](/troubleshooting/out-of-space).
5. **Restart and try once more.** If an earlier download is already waiting, a restart finishes it.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Check your internet connection and try again. | The update server could not be reached. Usually the connection, sometimes the server. |
| Updates cannot be reached yet (package access is private). | The update server is not open to the public yet. Nothing is wrong with your PC. Try again another day. |
| Ready. Restart to finish | Not an error. An update has downloaded and is waiting for a restart. |

> Note: OneClick OS is an early test version. During this period updates may sometimes be unavailable for reasons at our end.

## The download stops part way

- Stay on the Update page for one attempt and watch the megabytes. If the number stops rising for several minutes, the connection has probably dropped.
- On a laptop, plug in the charger and stop it going to sleep during the download. See [Power, sleep and battery](/settings/power-and-battery).
- If you are on a mobile hotspot or a metered connection, note the size shown before you start. System updates can be large.

## The PC does not start properly after an update

The version you had before is still on the PC. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update), which includes what to do when the desktop does not appear.

## Still stuck

Please [report the problem](https://oneclickos.org/report). Include the exact words shown under **Something went wrong**. See [Find the details we ask for in a report](/troubleshooting/get-details-for-a-report).
