Some things were left out of OneClick OS on purpose. This page lists them, and is clear about where the promise ends.

## It does not track you

- OneClick OS collects no usage data. There is no telemetry.
- The Settings page that would have sent usage information was removed, and that kind of reporting is switched off.
- When an app closes unexpectedly, you see a plain window with **Reopen**, **Copy Details** and **Close**. Nothing is uploaded. The details go somewhere only if you copy them and send them yourself.

## It does not need an account

You do not sign in to OneClick OS with an email address or an online account. The account you create during setup exists only on your PC.

You only sign in to an online service if you choose to use one, such as connecting OneDrive or Google Drive.

## It does not force updates

Nothing downloads, installs or restarts for an update unless you ask. See [Why OneClick never forces an update](/updates/why-no-forced-updates).

## It has no AI built in

No AI assistant is built into the system, and nothing on your PC is read or sent to an AI service by the system.

The Start menu has **Claude** and **ChatGPT** shortcuts. These only open those websites in your browser, the same as typing the address yourself. Nothing is installed for them. If you do not want them, right-click each one and choose **Unpin from Start**. See [The Claude and ChatGPT shortcuts](/apps/claude-and-chatgpt).

## Where the promise ends

This list is about the system itself. It does not cover:

- **Apps you install.** Each app has its own makers and its own privacy policy.
- **Websites.** What a website collects is between you and that site, whichever system you use.
- **Services you connect.** If you connect a cloud drive, that company handles your files under its own terms.
- **Things that need the internet to work.** Pressing **Check now** on the Update page contacts the update server to see whether a new version exists. Installing an app contacts the App Store's source. These are requests for a download, not reports about you.
