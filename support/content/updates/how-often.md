OneClick OS is an early test version, and there is no fixed timetable for updates yet. New versions are published when they are ready. During testing that can mean several in a short time and then a gap.

## How to find out if there is one

1. Open the **Start** menu and choose **Update**, or open **Settings** and choose **Update**.
2. Press **Check now**.

The check takes a few seconds and downloads nothing. You then see one of these:

- **You're up to date**, with the version you have.
- **A new version is available**, if you receive Full updates.
- **Security fixes are available**, if you receive Security only.

The PC does not download updates by itself, so checking is something you do.

## How often to check

Every week or two is a reasonable habit. Check sooner if:

- you reported a problem and are waiting for a fix
- you have heard about a serious security problem in the news
- something has stopped working and you want to see whether it has been fixed

## Two kinds of update

- **Full** updates bring new features and fixes as well as security fixes.
- **Security only** keeps your version and brings security fixes for it.

How often you see an update depends on which you chose. See [Full updates or security fixes only](/updates/full-or-security).

## Version numbers

Each version of OneClick OS has a short number, such as 0.1. You can see yours at the top of the **Update** page and in [About This PC](/settings/about-this-pc). Published earlier versions are listed under **Previous versions** on the Update page.

## Apps have their own updates

Apps come from the **App Store** and are updated by the people who make them, on their own timetable. They are not part of a system update. See [Update your apps](/apps/update-apps).
