summary: The difference between Download and Download and restart, and what happens at each step.

An update has two steps: downloading it, and restarting the PC so the new version is used. OneClick OS lets you separate the two, so an update never interrupts what you are doing.

## The two buttons

When the **Update** page shows **A new version is available**, you get two buttons:

- **Download** fetches the update while you carry on working. The new version is used the next time you restart, whenever you choose to do that.
- **Download and restart** fetches the update and restarts the PC as soon as the download has finished.

You are asked for your password before the download starts.

> Warning: **Download and restart** restarts without asking again once the download finishes. Save your work first, or use **Download** if you are in the middle of something.

## While it downloads

The page shows **Downloading...** with:

- a progress bar
- how many megabytes have arrived out of the total
- roughly how long is left, such as "about 4 min left"

You can keep using your PC. Nothing changes until you restart. You can close Settings and the download carries on.

## When the download finishes

If you pressed **Download**, the page changes to **Ready. Restart to finish** and a **Restart now** button appears.

You have two options:

- Press **Restart now**. Your open apps will close, so save your work first.
- Do nothing. Keep working for an hour or a week. The next time you restart or turn the PC on after shutting down, it starts on the new version.

There is no reminder that keeps coming back and no deadline.

## After the restart

The PC starts on the new version. Your files, apps and settings are as you left them. The version you had before is kept, so you can go back. See [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).

## If you change your mind after downloading

A downloaded update waits for the next restart. If you would rather not have it, restart, then use **Undo the last update** to return to the version you had.
