OneClick OS never updates behind your back. Nothing downloads, installs or restarts until you ask for it.

## Check for an update

1. Open the **Start menu** and choose **Update**. You can also open **Settings** and pick **Update**.
2. Press **Check for updates**. This takes a few seconds and downloads nothing.
3. If there is a new version, you see its name and its size in megabytes.

## Download it your way

You get two buttons:

- **Download** fetches the update in the background. You keep working, and the update is used the next time you restart, whenever that is.
- **Download and restart** fetches the update and restarts as soon as it is ready. You are asked for your password first.

While it downloads you see a progress bar, how many megabytes have arrived, and roughly how long is left.

> Note: You can keep using the computer during a download. Nothing changes on screen until you restart.

## Full or security only

Under **What to install** you choose one of two:

- **Full** gives you new features, fixes and security fixes.
- **Security fixes only** keeps the system as it is and installs only fixes for security problems.

You can change this at any time. It applies from the next check.

## Apps update separately

This page updates the system. Your apps are updated in the **App Store**, under **Updates**. See [Update your apps](/apps/update-apps).

## If an update goes wrong

Every update keeps the version you had before. If something stops working, see [Undo an update](/updates/undo-an-update).
